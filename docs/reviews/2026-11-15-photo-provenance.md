## Rejected candidates

These had acceptable licenses but did not match the recipe closely enough to use:

- Cheesecake (`New_York_cheesecake_3.jpg`): strawberry glaze and decorations; the recipe is plain.
- Broccoli rabe (`Rapini.jpg`): raw bunch, not the cooked dish.
- Olvera Street taquitos (`Taquitos - panoramio.jpg`): the file shows a pig's head.
- Fried biscuits (Carthay): dark photo of a different restaurant's biscuits, no apple butter.
- Smoked mullet dip (USFWS): the dip is barely visible.
- Key lime pie (Flickr, Steve Snodgrass): a drawing, not a photo.
- Key lime pie (Flickr, Benimoto): meringue topping; the recipe uses whipped cream.
- Fried whole-belly clams (Flickr, wickenden): good match but only 500 px; kept as a backup.
