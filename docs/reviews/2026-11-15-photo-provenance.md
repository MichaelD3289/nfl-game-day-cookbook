## Rejected candidates

These had acceptable licenses but did not match the recipe:

- Cheesecake (`New_York_cheesecake_3.jpg`): strawberry glaze and decorations; the recipe is plain.
- Izzy's potato pancakes (`Potato_pancakes.jpg`): thin latkes; the recipe is thick.
- Hot Baltimore crab dip (`Crab_dip_and_old_bay_chips.jpg`, `Crab_dip.jpg`): one is cold with no cheese top; the other has a whole crab on top.
- Broccoli rabe (`Rapini.jpg`): raw bunch, not the cooked dish.
- Lexington red slaw and maque choux: each is one item on a mixed plate.
- Olvera Street taquitos (`Taquitos - panoramio.jpg`): the file shows a pig's head.
- Walla Walla onion rings (Burgerville): crumb-breaded; the recipe is beer-battered.
- Fried biscuits (Carthay): dark photo of a different restaurant's biscuits, no apple butter.
- Smoked mullet dip (USFWS): the dip is barely visible.
- Mexican red rice: beans are prominent.
- Creamed spinach (Pappas Bros.): breadcrumb gratin topping not in the recipe.
- Detroit Greek salad: a coney dog fills half the crop.
