# Community code of conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
constructive, and patient. Discuss recipes and evidence without attacking people.
Credit others' work, accept corrections, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and targeted
personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, or other project spaces.

For abusive content, use GitHub's Report content option. For a private project report,
use the repository's private security-reporting form linked in SECURITY.md and mark it
as a conduct report. Include only the information needed to investigate.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close conversations, or restrict participation
based on severity and repetition. Reports will be handled discreetly; confidentiality
cannot be guaranteed where disclosure is needed to address a threat or GitHub policy.
These expectations also apply to maintainers.
