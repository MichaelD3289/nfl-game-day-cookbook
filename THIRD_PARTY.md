# Third-party material and attribution

This project contains recipes and images sourced from other websites. Original source
URLs and photo credits appear in recipe/component front matter and generated outputs.
Those credits are provenance, not a representation that redistribution rights have
been verified. A complete rights audit of existing material has not been performed.

The project's MIT and CC BY 4.0 grants do not relicense third-party material. Before
redistributing such material, establish the applicable license, permission, or other
basis for reuse. Do not assume that an image is freely reusable because it is public
on a recipe site or appears in this cookbook. Dependencies retain their own licenses.

## New contributions

For third-party material, include in the PR:

- The exact asset/content and original source URL.
- Creator/copyright owner and required attribution.
- License name and URL, or evidence of permission covering the intended distribution.
- Any modifications and conditions that must be preserved.

If permission is unclear, provide a source link or your own original replacement rather
than copying the text or image. Use the supported photo-credit field for public credits;
do not add unsupported recipe metadata. Permission evidence can be reviewed privately
if sharing it publicly would expose personal correspondence.

## Corrections and rights concerns

Open an issue identifying the material and source, or use the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/MichaelD3289/nfl-game-day-cookbook/security/advisories/new)
for sensitive details. The maintainer can review, replace, or remove disputed material.
