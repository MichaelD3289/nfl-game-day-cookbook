## Ingredients

- 1 3-pound chicken
- 1 pound lean pork
- 1 pound lean beef
- 3 medium onions, chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Water, to cover the meat
- 4 (16-ounce) cans diced tomatoes
- 5 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 1/2 (14-ounce) bottles catsup
- 1 tablespoon Tabasco
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/2 (12-ounce) bottle chili sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon dry mustard
- 1/2 stick butter
- 3 tablespoons vinegar
- 2 (16-ounce) cans small lima or butter beans
- 2 (16-ounce) cans cream-style corn
- 1 (15-ounce) can small English peas
- Optional: 3 small potatoes, peeled and diced
- Optional: 1 box frozen sliced okra

## Instructions

1. Put the chicken, pork and beef in a large, heavy pot. Season with salt and pepper, add the onions and cover with water.
2. Cook until the meat falls from the bones, several hours. Cool, shred the meat and return it to the stock.
3. Add the tomatoes, Worcestershire sauce, catsup, Tabasco, bay leaves, chili sauce, dry mustard and butter. Cook for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
4. Stir in the vinegar, lima beans, cream-style corn and peas. Add the optional potatoes and okra if using.
5. Cook slowly until thick, stirring to prevent sticking.

## Kitchen Notes

The Golden Isles visitor guide says Brunswick stew originated in Brunswick, Georgia, and serves it with barbecue or local seafood.
