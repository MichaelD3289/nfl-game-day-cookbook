## Ingredients

- 1 smoked ham hock
- 3 1/2 cups chicken stock
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes
- 1 cup Coca-Cola Original
- 1/2 cup lightly packed light brown sugar
- 1/2 small onion, cut into matchsticks
- 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1/3 cup Worcestershire sauce
- 2 small cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 1/2 teaspoons hot sauce, or to taste
- 1 large bunch (2 to 3 pounds) collard greens, stems removed and leaves coarsely chopped
- Morton kosher salt and black pepper (optional)

## Instructions

1. Put the ham hock in a stockpot and cover with the chicken stock, tomatoes, Coca-Cola, brown sugar, onion, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, garlic and hot sauce.
2. Bring to a boil over high heat and add the collard greens. Stir to combine and wilt the greens.
3. Reduce the heat to a simmer and cook for 30 to 45 minutes, until the greens are tender.
4. Taste and add more hot sauce and a pinch of salt or pepper, if desired.
5. Remove the ham hock, pull off the meat and add it to the greens. Discard the bones and skin.

## Kitchen Notes

The Atlanta Journal-Constitution reports that Coca-Cola braised collards are a staple side at Southern Bistro in nearby Sandy Springs.
