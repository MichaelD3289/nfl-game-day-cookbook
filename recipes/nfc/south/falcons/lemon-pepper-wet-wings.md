## Ingredients

- 3 pounds chicken wings, segmented (about 36 wing sections)
- 3 large eggs
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1 tablespoon hot sauce
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt for egg wash
- Vegetable oil, about 3 inches deep in pot, for frying

### Seasoned flour

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 2 tablespoons lemon-pepper seasoning
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder

### Wet sauce

- 1 cup unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon zest
- 2 teaspoons lemon-pepper seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Heat about 3 inches vegetable oil in a Dutch oven to 350°F. Beat eggs, milk, 1 tablespoon salt and hot sauce in a large bowl. In a separate bowl or bag, combine flour, cornstarch, baking powder, lemon-pepper seasoning, paprika, 1 tablespoon salt, garlic powder and onion powder.
2. Dip wings in egg mixture in batches, then coat 5 to 6 at a time in seasoned flour. Let coated wings rest on a plate while oil heats.
3. Set a wire rack over a rimmed sheet. Fry 5–6 wings per batch at 350–360°F, about 7 minutes per batch, until golden and 165°F in the thickest piece. Drain on the rack; let oil recover between batches.
4. Melt butter and whisk with lemon juice, zest, lemon-pepper seasoning, garlic powder and 1 teaspoon salt. Toss hot wings with all of the sauce for the wet finish; serve immediately with any extra sauce for dipping.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: The source lists 45 minutes total, but 36 wing sections fried 5–6 at a time need about 6–7 batches, so practical elapsed time is usually over an hour. The fried wings receive a measured lemon-pepper butter sauce; cook each batch to 165°F.
