## Ingredients

- 10 ears fresh corn
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 (10-ounce) can Ro-Tel tomatoes, with liquid
- 1 1/2 cups chopped onion
- 1/2 cup chopped green bell pepper
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Cut the tops of the kernels from the corn with a sharp knife, then scrape the cobs with the back of the knife to release the corn milk.
2. Melt the butter in a 3-quart microwave-safe dish. Add the onion, bell pepper and garlic and microwave on high until tender, about 3 minutes.
3. Stir in the corn, tomatoes and salt. Cover with wax paper and microwave on high for 20 minutes, stirring once or twice.

## Kitchen Notes

The LSU AgCenter collection calls maquechou an Indian-style stewed corn and tomato dish and credits this recipe to Jean K. Durkee.
