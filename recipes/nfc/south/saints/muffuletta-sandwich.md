## Ingredients

### Olive salad

- 1 1/4 cups coarsely chopped cauliflower florets
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 small carrots, roughly chopped
- 2 small celery ribs, thinly sliced
- 3 tablespoons water
- 3/4 cup chopped pitted green niçoise olives (or another briny green olive)
- 1/2 cup chopped pitted Kalamata olives
- 1/2 cup chopped roasted red peppers
- 1/4 cup drained sliced pickled banana peppers
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar

- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 round 8–10-inch Italian sesame-seed loaf (muffuletta loaf if available)
- 6 ounces thinly sliced deli ham
- 6 ounces thinly sliced Genoa salami
- 6 ounces thinly sliced mortadella
- 6 ounces thinly sliced provolone

## Instructions

1. In a 2-quart saucepan, bring cauliflower, olive oil, oregano, thyme, carrots, celery and 3 tablespoons water to a boil over medium-high heat. Cover, lower heat and simmer 10–12 minutes, just until vegetables soften.
2. Transfer vegetables and oil to a bowl. Stir in green and Kalamata olives, roasted peppers, banana peppers and vinegar. Taste before adding salt; olives are already salty. Cool completely.
3. Halve the round sesame loaf horizontally and pull a little soft bread from both halves to make room. Spread half the olive salad over the bottom half.
4. Layer ham, Genoa salami, mortadella and provolone; cover with remaining olive salad and the bread top. Wrap tightly and refrigerate 8 hours or overnight so the oil and brine season the bread. Cut into four wedges.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: The New Orleans sandwich is built on a round sesame loaf with deli meats, provolone and a marinated olive-and-pickled-vegetable salad. Allow the overnight rest for the briny oil to season the bread.
