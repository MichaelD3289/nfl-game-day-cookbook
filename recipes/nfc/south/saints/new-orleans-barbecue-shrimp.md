## Ingredients

- 3 pounds medium to large shrimp in the shell, preferably head-on, washed well; or 1 1/2 pounds deheaded shrimp in the shell
- 2 sticks butter or margarine
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1 cup minced onion
- 1/2 cup minced celery
- 1/2 cup minced bell pepper
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic
- 6 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- Juice of 1 lemon, plus 1/2 lemon thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon Louisiana hot sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons Creole seasoning
- 2 teaspoons coarsely ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tablespoon minced parsley, for garnishing
- Warm crusty French or Italian bread, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Pat the shrimp dry and place them in a large ovenproof baking dish.
2. Cook the butter, olive oil, onion, celery, bell pepper and garlic in a skillet over medium heat until the vegetables soften, 4 to 6 minutes.
3. Stir in the Worcestershire sauce, lemon juice, hot sauce, Creole seasoning, black pepper, salt and bay leaves. Pour the mixture over the shrimp and stir in the lemon slices.
4. Bake, stirring occasionally, until the shrimp are just cooked through and beginning to pull from their shells, 14 to 18 minutes.
5. Cool slightly, garnish with parsley and serve with warm crusty bread for dipping in the sauce.

## Kitchen Notes

Emeril Lagasse's recipe describes this as a New Orleans barbecue shrimp preparation and notes its shell-on shrimp and buttery sauce.
