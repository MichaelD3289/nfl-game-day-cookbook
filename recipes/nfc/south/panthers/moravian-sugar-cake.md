## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2 (1/4-ounce) packages active dry yeast
- 2/3 cup plain, unseasoned mashed potatoes
- 1 stick unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1/4 cup vegetable shortening
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 large eggs, slightly beaten
- 3 1/2 to 4 cups flour
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1 cup hot tap water

### Topping

- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 3 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1 stick unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled

## Instructions

1. Butter three 9-inch cake pans. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water and set aside until foamy, about 10 minutes.
2. Mix the mashed potatoes, hot water, butter, shortening, sugar and salt. Stir in the eggs and dissolved yeast, then gradually add the flour until the dough resembles a heavy muffin batter. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 1/2 hours.
3. Punch down the dough, divide it among the pans, cover and let rise until it reaches the top of the pans, about 30 minutes.
4. Heat the oven to 375°F. Stir together the brown sugar, cinnamon and melted butter for the topping.
5. Make indentations about 1 inch apart with your thumb, pressing to the bottom of the pans. Drizzle with the topping and bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until light brown.

## Kitchen Notes

Carolina Country ties the cake to Winston-Salem's Moravian community and Old Salem.
