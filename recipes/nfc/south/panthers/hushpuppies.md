## Ingredients

- 12 eggs
- 6 1/4 cups buttermilk
- 3 tablespoons plus 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup chopped scallions
- 21 finely chopped jalapeños, seeds are fine
- 1 1/2 cups melted butter
- 9 cups grated sharp white cheddar cheese
- 12 cups white cornmeal
- Oil for frying, deep enough for the hushpuppies to float
- Cocktail sauce or softened butter, for serving

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs into the buttermilk in a large bowl. Whisk in the salt, scallions, jalapeños and melted butter.
2. Fold in the cheese, then the cornmeal. Cover and refrigerate the mixture for 30 minutes.
3. Heat oil to 365°F, deep enough for the hushpuppies to float.
4. Drop walnut-sized portions into the hot oil and fry for 2 to 3 minutes, until browned and cooked through. Drain on paper towels and repeat with the remaining batter.
5. Serve with cocktail sauce or softened butter.

## Kitchen Notes

Bill Smith's recipe is from Crook's Corner in Chapel Hill, North Carolina.
