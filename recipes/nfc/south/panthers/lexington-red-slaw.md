## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup ketchup
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 teaspoons mild hot sauce
- 2 teaspoons ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 medium head cabbage, cored and roughly chopped

## Instructions

1. Whisk the ketchup, sugar, vinegar, hot sauce, black pepper and salt together in a large bowl.
2. Add the cabbage and toss to coat.
3. Let stand for 20 minutes, tossing occasionally.
4. Refrigerate until chilled, at least 2 hours.

## Kitchen Notes

Saveur describes Lexington's slaw as a ketchup-based cabbage dressing sharpened with cider vinegar and hot sauce.
