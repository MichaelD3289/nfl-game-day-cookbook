## Ingredients

- 2 slices North Carolina livermush (Hunter’s or Jenkins if available), each about 1/4 inch thick
- 2 slices sourdough bread
- 2 teaspoons stone-ground or yellow mustard
- 1 tablespoon grape jelly or jalapeño-onion grape jam
- 1 tablespoon neutral canola oil for pan-frying

## Instructions

1. Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the livermush until crisp, about 3 minutes on each side.
2. Toast the bread while the livermush cooks. Spread mustard on one slice and grape jelly or jam on the other.
3. Set crisp livermush between the slices and serve immediately.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Toast bread while livermush fries; about 10–15 minutes elapsed if ready-made livermush.
