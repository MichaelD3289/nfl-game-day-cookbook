## Ingredients

- 8 ounces sharp yellow cheddar, freshly shredded
- 4 ounces pepper Jack cheese, freshly shredded (for mild heat use Monterey Jack as an editorial substitution)
- 3/4 cup mayonnaise, preferably Duke’s for a Carolinas-style tang (brand is personal preference)
- 1/2 cup chopped drained pimientos
- 1 small shallot, grated
- Pinch of cayenne pepper, to preferred heat
- 8 slices soft white sandwich bread

## Instructions

1. Drain the pimientos thoroughly. Grate the cheeses rather than using preshredded cheese for a smoother spread.
2. Stir cheddar, pepper Jack, mayonnaise, pimientos, grated shallot and cayenne until evenly combined. Taste; adjust cayenne only if desired.
3. Spread one quarter of the mixture over each of four bread slices. Close with the remaining slices and cut in halves or quarters. Serve immediately, or chill the spread separately and assemble shortly before eating.
