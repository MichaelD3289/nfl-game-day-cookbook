## Ingredients

- One 9-inch piece Cuban bread
- 4 ounces thinly sliced smoked ham
- 1 1/2 ounces thinly sliced pork loin
- 1 ounce thinly sliced Genoa salami
- 1 ounce Swiss cheese
- 2 pickle chips
- 1 tablespoon yellow mustard {{component:american-yellow-mustard}}

## Instructions

1. Cut Cuban bread lengthwise. Layer ham, pork, salami and Swiss cheese in that order; arrange pickle chips on top.
2. If pork is cold, warm slices briefly before assembly so the pressed sandwich heats evenly. Lightly butter the outside only if desired; the Columbia source does not require it.
3. Spread mustard on the upper half of the bread. Press the sandwich until crisp, then cut diagonally.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Estimated 5–10 minutes assembly with cooked sliced pork; allow additional press time.
