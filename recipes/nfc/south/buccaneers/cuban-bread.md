## Ingredients

- 1 US quart water
- 1 ounce salt
- 2 ounces fast-action yeast, or 4 ounces dry yeast, or 8 ounces fresh yeast
- 1 ounce sugar
- 3 1/2 pounds strong white bread flour
- 3 ounces vegetable shortening or lard
- Palm leaf strips, for scoring

## Instructions

1. Knead the water, salt, yeast, sugar, flour and shortening or lard for 15 minutes. Let the dough rise for 45 minutes.
2. Divide the dough into 15 balls of about 6 ounces and let rise for 45 minutes.
3. Combine three balls to shape each of five elongated loaves. Wet palm leaf strips and press them into the top of the loaves.
4. Place the loaves leaf side down on the baking surface, cover and let rise for 45 minutes. Bake at 400°F for 20 to 30 minutes.

## Kitchen Notes

La Segunda Bakery's official site identifies its bakery in Tampa's Ybor City and its Cuban bread as a Tampa product. A commenter on the recipe page reports making 15 six-ounce dough balls and combining three per loaf; the author replies that loaf size can vary.
