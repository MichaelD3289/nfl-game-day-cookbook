## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound dried garbanzo beans (chickpeas)
- 2 quarts water
- 1 tablespoon salt for soaking
- 1 ham bone
- 1 beef bone
- 1/4 pound finely diced salt pork
- 1 finely chopped onion
- 2 potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- Pinch of saffron threads, crushed and bloomed in a spoonful of hot broth
- Salt to taste
- 1 dry-cured Spanish chorizo sausage, sliced thinly (smoked paprika style; not raw Mexican chorizo)

## Instructions

1. Sort and rinse dried chickpeas. Cover with plenty of water and 1 tablespoon salt; soak overnight, then drain.
2. In a 4-quart or larger soup pot, cover chickpeas, ham bone and beef bone with 2 quarts fresh water. Simmer gently 45 minutes, skimming foam. Test chickpeas; keep simmering until nearly tender before adding potatoes.
3. In a skillet, slowly render 1/4 pound diced salt pork. Add finely chopped onion and sauté until translucent; transfer to soup.
4. Add quartered potatoes, 1/2 teaspoon paprika and crushed saffron threads bloomed in a spoonful of hot soup. Simmer until potatoes and chickpeas are tender; taste before adding any salt.
5. Remove bones, return any usable ham meat to pot, and stir in thin rounds of dry-cured Spanish chorizo. Warm the soup through for several minutes; serve hot.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Requires overnight chickpea soak; later simmer is not quantified.
