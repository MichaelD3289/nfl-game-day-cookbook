## Ingredients

### Shortcakes

- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, sifted
- 6 tablespoons granulated sugar, divided
- 4 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- Finely grated zest of 1 lemon
- 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes
- 3/4 cup half-and-half or whole milk
- 2 tablespoons melted butter

### Strawberries and cream

- 2 pints (4 cups) fresh strawberries, hulled and sliced
- 1 cup heavy cream, chilled
- 2 tablespoons sour cream, crème fraîche or mascarpone (optional)
- 2 tablespoons confectioners' sugar
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Combine the flour, 2 tablespoons sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt and lemon zest. Cut in the cold butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
2. Stir in the half-and-half until barely combined. Pat the dough 1/2 inch thick and cut out 3-inch rounds, handling the dough as little as possible.
3. Butter or spray a baking sheet. Brush half the rounds with melted butter and stack them on the remaining rounds. Brush the tops with milk or half-and-half, sprinkle with 2 tablespoons sugar and bake about 15 minutes, until light golden.
4. Toss the strawberries with the remaining 2 tablespoons sugar, lightly crush some berries and let stand at least 15 minutes.
5. Beat the heavy cream, optional sour cream, confectioners' sugar and vanilla to stiff peaks. Split each biscuit and layer with strawberries and cream.

## Kitchen Notes

Parkesdale Farm Market in Plant City describes its shortcake as sliced fresh berries served over shortcake with whipped cream. Use Plant City strawberries when available.
