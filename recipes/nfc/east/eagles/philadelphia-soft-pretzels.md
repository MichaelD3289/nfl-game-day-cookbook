## Ingredients

- Pretzel or kosher salt for topping
- Optional: homemade American yellow mustard, for dipping; or substitute store-bought yellow mustard {{component:american-yellow-mustard}}

### Dough

- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 cups room-temperature water
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- 3 cups (340 g) King Arthur Golden Wheat flour (or comparable whole-wheat blend)
- 2 teaspoons non-diastatic malt powder or sugar
- 1 tablespoon table salt
- 2 1/2 to 3 cups bread flour

### Bath

- 6 cups water
- 2 tablespoons baking soda

## Instructions

1. Mix sugar, water and yeast, then add Golden Wheat flour, malt, salt and enough bread flour to make a soft nonsticky dough. Knead and let rise until puffy, about 1 hour.
2. Divide into 16 pieces, roll into ropes and twist into pretzels. Heat oven to 450°F and prepare baking sheet.
3. Boil bath water with baking soda. Immerse pretzels four at a time for 1 minute, transfer to sheet and sprinkle with salt.
4. Bake 12 to 15 minutes until deeply browned.
5. Drain each boiled pretzel well before placing on the baking sheet. Bake until uniformly deep brown; cool a few minutes before eating.
