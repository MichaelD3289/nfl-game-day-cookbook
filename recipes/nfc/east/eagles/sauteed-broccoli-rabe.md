## Ingredients

- 1 large bunch broccoli rabe (about 2 lbs), tough ends trimmed
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 medium cloves garlic, minced or pressed through a garlic press
- Pinch crushed red pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon salt, for the blanching water

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil (1 teaspoon salt, enough water to submerge the rabe). Add the rabe and cook about 1 minute, until bright green. Fill a large bowl with ice water.
2. Lift the rabe out with tongs into the ice water to remove some of the bitterness. When completely cool, remove and dry in a salad spinner or on a clean kitchen towel.
3. Heat the olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and sauté 30 seconds, until fragrant.
4. Add the rabe and toss with the garlic and oil. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 8 minutes.
5. Sprinkle with the crushed red pepper and salt to taste. Serve immediately.

## Kitchen Notes

Philadelphia Magazine calls broccoli rabe the green in the "pork with greens" option at Tony Luke's and the bitter bite that pairs with tender roast pork. It also suits meatballs, grains and pasta.
