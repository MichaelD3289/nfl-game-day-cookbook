## Ingredients

- 12 corn tortillas
- 1/2 cup olive or canola oil
- 2 large yellow onions
- 3 cups grated Longhorn or medium yellow cheddar, plus extra for topping if available (source does not state extra quantity)

### For chili gravy

- 2 tablespoons shortening or lard
- 2 1/2 tablespoons masa harina
- 2 tablespoons mild Tex-Mex chili powder blend (ground dried chiles with cumin/garlic; not pure cayenne)
- 2 cups warm water
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Make the chili gravy: melt shortening or lard in a skillet, stir in masa harina and cook a few minutes until lightly browned. Add chili powder, warm water and salt; cook, stirring, until thickened. Keep warm.
2. Heat oven to 400°F and lightly grease a 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Finely chop the two yellow onions and grate the Longhorn/cheddar. Reserve some cheese for the top before filling tortillas.
3. Heat oil in a separate skillet over medium heat. Briefly dip each tortilla in the hot oil, about 2–3 seconds, to soften; let excess oil drip off.
4. Coat both sides of each tortilla in chili gravy. Add grated cheese and onion, roll it up, and place seam-side down in the baking dish. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
5. Pour remaining gravy over the enchiladas, top generously with more cheese and bake about 10 minutes, until bubbly. Serve promptly.
