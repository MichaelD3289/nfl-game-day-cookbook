## Ingredients

- 1 pound dried black-eyed peas or 2 (15-ounce) cans black-eyed peas, drained and rinsed
- Kosher salt
- 1/2 medium red onion, cut into thin slivers
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 jalapeño, cut into rings (optional)
- 1/2 to 3/4 cup canola or olive oil
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. If starting with dried peas, place the peas and about 1 tablespoon salt in a large pot, cover with 2 inches of water, and soak overnight, or bring to a boil, turn off the heat and cover for 1 hour.
2. Bring the soaked peas to a boil, turn the heat down to low and simmer covered for 30 minutes. Uncover and simmer until tender, about 1 hour. Drain. (With canned peas, skip steps 1 and 2.)
3. Place the cooked peas in a bowl and stir in the onion, garlic and jalapeño.
4. Whisk together the oil and vinegar, pour over the peas and stir until combined. Add the pepper, then salt to taste, about 1/2 teaspoon.
5. Chill covered for at least 4 hours before serving; they get tangier the longer they sit and keep a week in the refrigerator.

## Kitchen Notes

Homesick Texan adapts this from Helen Corbitt's recipe, which she first called pickled black-eyed peas, and says Neiman Marcus, where Corbitt began working in 1955, sold the peas in jars. Corbitt recommended serving them with a plate and fork, though they can also be a garnish or a dip.
