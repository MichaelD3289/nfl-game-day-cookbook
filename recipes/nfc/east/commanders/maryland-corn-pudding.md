## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon flour
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup grated corn
- Butter, for dotting the top

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a baking dish.
2. Beat the eggs. Add the salt, flour and sugar.
3. Whisk in the milk and grated corn.
4. Pour into the greased dish and dot with butter.
5. Bake until the pudding is solid but wobbly, about 45 minutes.

## Kitchen Notes

Old Line Plate adapts this recipe from Maryland's Way: The Hammond-Harwood House Cookbook, by Betty Worthington Briscoe, who wrote a weekly "Know Your County" column for the Calvert Independent.
