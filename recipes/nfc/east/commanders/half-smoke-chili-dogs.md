## Ingredients

- 6 smoked beef-and-pork half-smoke sausages (such as the half-smokes sold by Ben's Chili Bowl, if available)
- 6 soft hot dog buns
- ½ white onion, finely chopped
- Yellow mustard {{component:american-yellow-mustard}}

### Chili spice mix

- 3 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 3 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon cayenne (or to taste)
- 1½ teaspoons each garlic powder, onion powder and dried oregano
- ½ teaspoon each mustard powder and black pepper

### Chili

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- ½ onion, finely chopped
- ½ red bell pepper, finely chopped
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 (14-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 2 beef bouillon cubes
- 1½ cups water
- ¾ teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste

## Instructions

1. Mix the spices. Heat oil in a heavy pot over medium-high. Cook garlic, onion and bell pepper 3 minutes. Add beef; break into fine crumbs and cook until no pink remains. Stir in tomato paste for 1 minute.
2. Add spice mix, crushed tomatoes, crumbled bouillon, water and salt. Bring to a simmer, lower heat, partially cover and cook 3 hours, stirring occasionally. Briefly blend 1½ cups chili and stir it back in to thicken; keep warm.
3. Grill or pan-sear half-smokes over medium to medium-high heat until browned and heated through; follow the package temperature guidance if sausages are uncooked. Warm or steam buns.
4. Put one sausage in each bun and top with yellow mustard, chopped white onion and chili.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: This is a homemade beanless chili topping for smoked beef-and-pork half-smokes, not a claim to recreate Ben's proprietary chili formula. D.C.'s classic assembly uses the half-smoke with chili, yellow mustard and chopped onion; cheese is not included in the default. The tomato and pepper quantities remain those of the measured chili-dog source because no measured local Ben's-style chili formula was located.
