## Ingredients

### Wings

- 2 pounds chicken wings, separated into drumettes and flats
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 1 tablespoon Louisiana-style cayenne hot sauce
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon each paprika, garlic powder and onion powder
- ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper
- Salt and black pepper
- Neutral oil for deep-frying

### Mumbo sauce

- 1 cup ketchup
- ¼ cup distilled white vinegar
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- 6 ounces pineapple juice (¾ cup)
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 2 tablespoons Louisiana-style cayenne hot sauce
- 1 tablespoon each Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce, tomato paste and Dijon or spicy brown mustard
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- ½ teaspoon each onion powder and paprika
- Pinch of cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Mix buttermilk and 1 tablespoon hot sauce; add wings, cover and refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight. Take out 30 minutes before frying.
2. Mix flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, salt and pepper.
3. Heat oil in a deep fryer or heavy pot to 350°F. Lift wings from the buttermilk, dredge in seasoned flour and shake off the excess.
4. Fry in batches for 8–10 minutes, until golden and 165°F inside. Drain; let the oil return to 350°F between batches.
5. Meanwhile, simmer all sauce ingredients over medium-low heat for 8–10 minutes, until slightly thickened. Adjust sweetness or heat to taste.
6. Drizzle warm mumbo sauce over the wings and serve.

## Kitchen Notes

Mumbo (or mambo) sauce has been a D.C. carryout staple since the 1960s, served on wings and fries. The D.C. version is thinner and sweeter than Chicago's original. Ketchup, vinegar, sugar or pineapple juice and hot sauce define it; recipes vary.
