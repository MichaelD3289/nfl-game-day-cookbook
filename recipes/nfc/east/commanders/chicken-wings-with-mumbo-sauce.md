## Ingredients

- 2 pounds chicken wings, separated into drumettes and flats and patted dry
- Neutral oil for deep-frying (enough for 2 inches in a heavy pot)
- ½ cup ketchup
- ½ cup cane or golden syrup
- 2 tablespoons water
- 2 tablespoons distilled white vinegar
- 1½ tablespoons Louisiana-style cayenne hot sauce
- 1½ teaspoons sweet paprika
- 1 tablespoon whiskey (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a small saucepan, simmer ketchup, syrup, water, vinegar, hot sauce, paprika and optional whiskey for 10–15 minutes, stirring, until slightly thickened. Cool slightly.
2. Heat 2 inches oil in a heavy pot to 350°F. Fry dry wing pieces in batches for 12–15 minutes, turning as needed, until golden and at least 165°F in the thickest part. Drain on a rack; return oil to 350°F between batches.
3. Toss hot wings with about half the mumbo sauce. Serve immediately with remaining sauce on the side.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: The linked Washington Post recipe supplies the complete wing-and-sauce dish. This version uses its measured sauce with a 350°F deep-fry method supported by the James Beard Foundation wing recipe, reflecting D.C. carryout service. Cook every batch to 165°F.
