## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for rolling
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon white vinegar
- 1/2 cup water

### Filling

- 1 1/2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 1 tablespoon vegetable or olive oil
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1/2 teaspoon table salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

### Egg wash

- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 teaspoon water

## Instructions

1. Stir the flour, baking powder and salt together in a bowl. Whisk the egg, oil, vinegar and water in a second bowl, pour over the dry ingredients and mix. When it is a craggy mass, knead until smooth, about 1 minute. Cover and rest for 1 hour.
2. Cover the potatoes with cold water, bring to a boil over high heat, then cook over medium heat until tender, about 20 minutes. Drain and cool.
3. Cook the onion in the oil and butter over medium heat, then medium-low, stirring often, until deeply caramelized, about 45 minutes. Mash with the potatoes and season with the salt and plenty of pepper.
4. Heat the oven to 375°F and line a baking sheet with parchment. Roll half the dough on a floured surface into a very thin sheet, about 1 foot square. Lay a 2-inch-thick log of half the filling along the bottom edge and roll the dough around it until it is wrapped twice. Trim the ends even with the filling.
5. Press indentations every 3 to 3 1/2 inches and twist the dough at each one like sausage links. Snip at each twist, pinch one end of each piece closed, and flatten with your palm into a squat shape. Repeat with the remaining dough and filling.
6. Whisk the egg yolk with the water and brush over the knishes. Bake, not touching, rotating the tray if needed, until golden brown, about 45 minutes.

## Kitchen Notes

Serve with spicy mustard, the traditional accompaniment. For the open-topped Houston Street look, press the dough over the filling and leave the tops mostly open.
