## Ingredients

### Salad

- 2 large tart apples (like Granny Smith)
- 2 stalks celery

### Mayonnaise

- 1 egg yolk
- 1/2 teaspoon ground mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 3/4 cup olive or canola oil

### To serve

- 4 large apples
- Boston or butterhead lettuce leaves, washed and dried

## Instructions

1. Peel the 2 tart apples and dice them into 1/2-inch pieces. Trim the celery and dice to the same size. Combine in a medium bowl.
2. Whisk the egg yolk, mustard, salt, pepper and lemon juice in a large deep bowl until bubbles form, 1 to 2 minutes. Add the oil a few drops at a time, whisking constantly.
3. When the mixture thickens, pour in the remaining oil in a steady stream, whisking.
4. Stir about 1/2 cup of the mayonnaise into the apples and celery, adding more to taste.
5. Hollow out the 4 large apples into thin-walled cups, zigzagging the rims if you like, and hold them in cold water. Drain and dry, fill with the salad and serve on lettuce leaves.

## Kitchen Notes

Table Magazine says Oscar Tschirky, maître d' of the Waldorf-Astoria, created the salad and included it in his 1896 cookbook, where the original was apples and celery "dressed with a good mayonnaise." The source recommends pasteurized eggs for the mayonnaise.
