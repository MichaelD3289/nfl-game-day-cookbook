## Ingredients

### Jalapeño sauce

- 4 jalapeños (about 8 ounces), stemmed and seeded for mild heat
- 1 garlic clove
- 1/2 medium white or yellow onion
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- Kosher salt, to taste

- 8 jumbo all-beef hot dogs (beef is Tucson-style recommendation; source says jumbo dogs)
- 8 regular-cut bacon slices
- 1 (15.5-ounce) can pinto beans, rinsed and drained
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil for onions
- 2 medium white onions, thinly sliced
- 8 soft bolillo-style Sonoran hot-dog rolls, or soft bolillos
- 2 medium tomatoes, finely chopped
- 2 avocados, peeled, pitted and chopped
- Mayonnaise and yellow mustard, for drizzling
- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. For jalapeño sauce, simmer jalapeños, garlic and half an onion under water about 15 minutes until soft. Drain and cool slightly; blend while streaming in 2 tablespoons oil until creamy. Salt to taste.
2. Wrap each hot dog in one regular-cut bacon slice. Warm drained pinto beans in a small pot over medium heat 3–5 minutes; season lightly and keep warm.
3. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium-high. Brown sliced white onions 7–8 minutes, then transfer to a plate. Lower skillet to medium and cook bacon-wrapped dogs, turning, about 7 minutes until bacon is crisp and sausages hot.
4. Steam rolls until soft: wrap each in a damp paper towel and microwave 20 seconds, or steam in a basket over simmering water for about 2 minutes.
5. Set a bacon-wrapped dog in each roll. Add pinto beans, onions, tomatoes and avocado; drizzle with jalapeño sauce, mayonnaise and yellow mustard.
