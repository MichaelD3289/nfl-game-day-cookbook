## Ingredients

- 4 medium flour tortillas (buy the thinnest ones you can find)
- 4 teaspoons unsalted butter, melted
- 2 cups grated cheeses (the author uses Longhorn-style cheddar and Oaxaca melting cheese; Monterey Jack and cheddar are also suggested)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. Brush the tortillas with butter on both sides. Set them on a wire rack over a baking sheet and bake 20 to 25 minutes, turning halfway through.
3. Remove the tortillas and spread the cheese over them.
4. Raise the oven to 450°F. Return the tortillas to the oven about 5 minutes, until the cheese bubbles.
