## Ingredients

### Chocolate dip

- 5 (3.1-ounce) disks Mexican chocolate, finely chopped
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream

### Churros

- 6 tablespoons (3/4 stick) butter, cut into pieces
- 1 cup water
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- Pinch of nutmeg
- 1 cup flour
- 2 eggs
- 2 inches oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Put chocolate and cream in a double boiler or metal bowl over simmering water. Stir until melted, about 15 to 20 minutes.
2. Bring 1 cup water to a boil with butter, sugar, salt and nutmeg. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer until the butter melts.
3. Remove from heat, add all the flour and beat vigorously. Return to the stove and beat over moderately high heat for 1 to 2 minutes, until the mixture leaves the pan sides, forms a mass and films the bottom.
4. Remove from heat. Beat in the eggs one at a time until absorbed, then beat 1 to 2 minutes more until smooth. Spoon into a pastry bag fitted with a 3/4-inch star tip.
5. Heat 2 inches of oil in a skillet to 350°F. Pipe strips or loops into the oil, cutting each with a spatula. Fry 3 to 4 at a time until dark golden, about 3 to 4 minutes; drain on paper towels.
6. Serve immediately with the chocolate dip.
