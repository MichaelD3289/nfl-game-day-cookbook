## Ingredients

- 4 all-beef hot dogs (other franks work by preference)
- 4 soft hot dog buns, warmed
- 4 slices bacon
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 2 jalapeños, halved
- 10 sprigs fresh cilantro, washed
- 2 teaspoons ketchup
- 2 teaspoons mustard
- 1 teaspoon mayonnaise
- About 2 teaspoons olive oil or rendered bacon fat for sautéing peppers and onions

## Instructions

1. Wrap each hot dog tightly in a slice of bacon.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium heat. Place the dogs seam-side down and cook about 7 minutes before turning; sprinkle lightly with sea salt or seasoning salt.
3. Cook the sliced pepper, onion and halved jalapeños over medium heat with about 2 teaspoons olive oil or rendered bacon fat; avoid burning the vegetables.
4. Turn the hot dogs until bacon is crisp on all sides, about 5 minutes per side. Warm the buns on the grill or pan.
5. Assemble with grilled vegetables and cilantro. Add ketchup, mustard and mayonnaise; guacamole is an optional topping.
