## Ingredients

### Beef filling

- 2 pounds beef chuck steak
- 2 tablespoons canola oil
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 2 jalapeño peppers, seeded and diced
- 4 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 2 tablespoons cumin seeds
- 1 orange, thinly sliced
- 1/3 cup fresh lime juice
- 1/4 cup white vinegar
- Salt and pepper
- Water, to cover steak

### Spicy avocado sauce

- 4 cups water
- 2 yellow sweet peppers
- 1 jalapeño pepper
- 2 large garlic cloves
- 6 tomatillos, husked and washed (just under 1 pound)
- Handful of fresh cilantro
- 1 avocado
- 2 teaspoons salt

### Taquitos

- 24 white corn tortillas
- 1 to 1 1/2 inches canola oil, for frying
- Toothpicks

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Season the steak with salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat and sear the steak for 3 minutes per side; remove it.
2. Reduce heat to medium. Sauté the onion and jalapeños until soft, about 8 minutes. Add garlic and cumin seeds and cook 1 to 2 minutes, until fragrant.
3. Add orange, lime juice, vinegar, and steak. Add water to cover, bring to a boil, cover, and bake 1 1/2 hours, or until the meat shreds easily. Shred the meat, discarding the other solids.
4. Put water, peppers, garlic, tomatillos and cilantro in a pot. Bring to a boil and simmer 5 minutes. Drain, reserving some water. Blend the cooked ingredients with avocado and salt, adding reserved water until the sauce is smooth and not too thick.
5. Heat 1 to 1 1/2 inches of oil in a skillet until shimmering. Fry tortillas for 5 seconds per side; drain on paper towels. Turn off the heat. Fill and roll each tortilla around some beef, securing with toothpicks.
6. Reheat the oil and fry taquitos in batches until crisp and golden. Drain, remove toothpicks, and serve with the avocado sauce.

## Kitchen Notes

Cielito Lindo’s taquitos on Olvera Street inspired this version. The source notes that its sauce uses yellow sweet peppers and jalapeño rather than the restaurant’s chile güero.
