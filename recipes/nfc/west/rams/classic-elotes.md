## Ingredients

- Salt
- 4 ears sweet corn
- 1 cup crumbled cotija cheese
- 1 tablespoon ground cayenne, or to taste
- 4 short corn skewers
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup (1/2 stick) butter, melted
- 1 lime, cut into wedges

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Remove the husks and silk from the corn and boil until the kernels are crisp, 5 to 10 minutes. Remove and cool.
2. Spread the cheese on a plate. Combine cayenne with salt to taste in a small bowl or shaker. Push a skewer through the bottom of each cob.
3. Spread mayonnaise over each cob and drizzle with melted butter. Roll in cheese, sprinkle with chili salt, squeeze a lime wedge over each, and serve.
