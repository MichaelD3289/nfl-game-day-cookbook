## Ingredients

- 1 (1.5-quart) container mint ice cream

### Oatmeal cookies

- 1 cup (5 ounces) all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 3 cups (9 ounces) old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 cup (3 ounces) raisins
- 3/4 cup packed (5 1/4 ounces) dark brown sugar
- 1/2 cup (3 1/2 ounces) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 2 teaspoons molasses
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 large egg, plus 1 large yolk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Chocolate coating

- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
- 1/4 cup unrefined coconut oil

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line two large sheet trays with parchment. Process the oats and raisins until finely ground. Whisk the flour, salt, and baking soda in a bowl.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the brown and granulated sugars, oil, melted butter, molasses, and cinnamon until smooth. Whisk in the egg, yolk, and vanilla, then mix in the flour mixture and ground oats and raisins until no dry bits remain.
3. Portion the dough into 24 cookies with a 1 1/2-tablespoon scoop, roll into balls, and flatten to 1/2 inch thick. Bake one tray at a time until the tops begin to crack, 5 to 7 minutes. Cool completely.
4. Slice the ice cream container into six slabs and cut a disc from each. Sandwich each disc between two cookies, cover, and freeze at least 1 hour or overnight.
5. Microwave the chocolate chips and coconut oil in 30-second increments, stirring until smooth, about 1 to 1 1/2 minutes total. Cool to room temperature; dip each frozen sandwich and chill until the coating sets, about 20 minutes.

## Kitchen Notes

The It’s-It company says George Whitney created the sandwich at San Francisco’s Playland-at-the-Beach; its original combines vanilla ice cream, oatmeal cookies, and dark chocolate. This recipe follows Kelly Djalali’s mint version.
