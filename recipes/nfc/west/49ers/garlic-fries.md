## Ingredients

- Nonstick vegetable oil spray
- 2 1/4 pounds russet potatoes, cut lengthwise into 3 x 1/3 x 1/3-inch batons
- 3 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons chopped flat-leaf parsley

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 450°F. Coat a large rimmed baking sheet with nonstick spray. Toss the potatoes with 2 1/2 tablespoons oil and season with salt and pepper; arrange in a single layer.
2. Roast, turning occasionally, until browned and tender, about 30 minutes. Raise the oven to 500°F and roast about 5 minutes more, until deep brown in spots.
3. Whisk the remaining 1/2 tablespoon oil with the garlic and parsley. Add the hot fries, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat.

## Kitchen Notes

The source calls these a lighter take on the Gilroy Garlic Fries at the San Francisco Giants' former AT&T Park.
