## Ingredients

### Carne asada

- 1 1/2 pounds flank or skirt steak

### Marinade

- 1/2 cup canola oil
- 1 orange, thinly sliced
- 1 small white onion, thinly sliced
- 1 seeded, diced jalapeño
- 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 2 tablespoons white vinegar
- 1/2 tablespoon each ground cumin, mild chili powder, ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon each garlic powder, onion powder and salt

### Mexican rice

- 1 cup long-grain white rice
- 1/4 yellow onion, diced, and 1 peeled garlic clove
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder
- 1 1/2 cups water, plus 1/4 cup for blending aromatics
- 2 tablespoons canola oil

### Beans

- 1 can pinto beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 1/2 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/3 cup diced onion and 1 peeled garlic clove
- 1 teaspoon mild chili powder; 1/2 teaspoon each paprika, cumin and black pepper; 1 bay leaf
- Optional 1/2 teaspoon chipotle powder; start with 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt (reduced from the source's 2 teaspoons), then adjust after simmering

### Pico

- 3–4 seeded, diced Roma tomatoes
- 1/4 red onion, diced; juice of 1 lime; 1–2 tablespoons minced cilantro; 1 minced jalapeño or serrano; salt to taste

- 3–4 very large flour tortillas (about 12–13 inches)
- Shredded Monterey Jack and cheddar, to taste
- 1 avocado, sliced
- Homemade roasted salsa verde, to taste; or substitute store-bought salsa verde {{component:roasted-salsa-verde}}
- Aluminum foil for the classic wrap

## Instructions

1. Whisk marinade oil, lime juice, vinegar and dry seasonings. Pour over steak with orange slices, white onion and jalapeño; marinate covered in the refrigerator 3–4 hours or overnight.
2. For rice, blend the 1/4 cup water with rice onion, garlic and tomato paste. Toast rice in 2 tablespoons oil over medium heat 3–5 minutes; stir in blended paste for 30–60 seconds. Add 1 1/2 cups water and bouillon, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 20 minutes; rest covered 10 minutes and fluff.
3. Simmer pinto beans with broth, onion, garlic, spices and bay leaf for about 45 minutes. Remove bay leaf and garlic; drain enough liquid that burritos will not leak, then taste for salt.
4. Mix pico tomatoes, red onion, lime, cilantro, jalapeño and salt; drain excess juice before assembly.
5. Discard marinade. Pat steak dry and sear in a hot oiled cast-iron pan or on a grill, about 3 minutes per side for a thin steak; use a thermometer for your preferred doneness. Rest 5–10 minutes, then slice against grain and chop.
6. Steam each oversized tortilla about 1 minute until pliable. In its center layer cheese, 1/3–1/2 pound chopped steak, pico, rice, drained beans and avocado; add homemade roasted salsa verde or prepared salsa to taste.
7. Fold in both sides, roll tightly, then wrap firmly in foil to hold the heavy filling. Eat from the foil sleeve.
