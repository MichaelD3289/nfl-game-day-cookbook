## Ingredients

- 1/4 small red onion, cut into chunks
- 1 tablespoon capers, drained
- 2 tablespoons fresh dill
- 8 ounces hot smoked salmon, skin removed
- 1 cup nonfat Greek yogurt
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 teaspoon prepared horseradish

## Instructions

1. Put onion, capers, and dill in a food processor fitted with a steel blade. Pulse 10 times to chop and combine.
2. Crumble salmon into the processor. Add yogurt, lemon juice, and horseradish.
3. Process 30 seconds, scraping down the bowl if needed, until combined.
