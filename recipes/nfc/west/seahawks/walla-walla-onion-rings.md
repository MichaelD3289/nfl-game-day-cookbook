## Ingredients

- 3 inches vegetable oil, for deep-frying
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/4 cups rice flour
- 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 tablespoon Montreal steak seasoning (not low-sodium)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
- 1 12-ounce bottle Belgian white beer, chilled
- 1/2 cup seltzer, chilled
- 2 sweet onions (such as Walla Walla), sliced into 1/2-inch-thick rings

## Instructions

1. Heat 3 inches of vegetable oil in a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat to 365°F. Meanwhile, heat oven to 200°F and set a rack on a rimmed baking sheet.
2. Whisk 1 cup all-purpose flour, rice flour, baking powder, steak seasoning, 1/2 teaspoon salt and a few grinds of pepper in a large bowl. Whisk in beer and seltzer until mostly smooth. Let stand 5 minutes to thicken. Put remaining 1/2 cup all-purpose flour in a shallow bowl.
3. Working in about five batches, dredge onion rings in flour, then dip in beer batter and let excess drip off. Fry until crisp and golden, flipping occasionally, 3 to 4 minutes.
4. Remove rings to the rack with a slotted spoon, season with salt, and keep warm in the oven while frying the remaining batches.
