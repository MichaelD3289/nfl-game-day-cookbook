## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large onion, thinly sliced
- 1 package (about 8) all-beef hot dogs; Applegate is the source brand, but any quality beef frank works
- 8 hot dog buns, preferably toasted
- 8 tablespoons cream cheese
- Yellow mustard, to taste; optional sliced pickled jalapeños for a common Seattle street-cart variation

## Instructions

1. Heat oil over medium-high heat and cook onion, stirring occasionally, until soft and golden, 8–10 minutes.
2. Grill or pan-sear beef franks until browned and hot. Split and toast buns lightly, then spread 1 tablespoon cream cheese inside each.
3. Top with softened onions and yellow mustard; add pickled jalapeños only if desired. Serve while cream cheese is warm and soft.
