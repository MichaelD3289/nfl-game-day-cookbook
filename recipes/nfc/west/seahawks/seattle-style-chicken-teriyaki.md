## Ingredients

- 2 pounds boneless, skin-on chicken thighs
- 1 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon mirin (sweet Japanese rice wine; not rice vinegar)
- 1 tablespoon sake (dry Japanese rice wine; omit only by preference)
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon grated ginger
- Sesame seeds, for garnish
- Optional cornstarch slurry for thicker glaze (source gives no amount; begin with 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed into 1 teaspoon cold water)

## Instructions

1. Debone and flatten the thighs, leaving the skin on.
2. Mix soy sauce, sugar, mirin, sake, garlic and ginger. Pour half over the chicken and marinate at least 30 minutes, up to overnight; reserve the rest.
3. Simmer the reserved sauce over medium heat to reduce. A cornstarch slurry may be added if a thicker sauce is preferred.
4. Grill skin-side down first for char, then turn and cook until the thickest thigh reaches 165°F. Rest 5 minutes, slice and serve over hot white rice with sauce; a small iceberg salad is the source's classic side.
5. Alternative air-fryer method: cook at 375°F for 14 minutes, flipping halfway; if sugar browns too quickly after 7 minutes, reduce heat to 350°F. Cook to 165°F internally.
