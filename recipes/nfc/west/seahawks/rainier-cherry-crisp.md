## Ingredients

### Topping

- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup rolled oats
- 3/4 cup sliced almonds
- 1/2 cup dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/8 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon pure almond extract
- 8 tablespoons butter, melted

### Filling

- 2 pounds Rainier cherries, pitted
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Combine topping ingredients in a medium bowl and set aside.
2. Grease the bottom of an oven-safe 10- to 12-inch skillet or baking dish.
3. Add cherries, sugar, cinnamon and flour; stir to coat. Cover the cherries completely with the topping.
4. Bake 40 to 45 minutes, until the top is golden and filling bubbly. Cool on a wire rack at least 15 minutes.
5. Serve warm or at room temperature with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
