## Ingredients

- 5 unpeeled red-skinned potatoes (about 2 pounds)
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 4 slices bacon or turkey bacon, diced
- 1/4 cup sugar or equivalent sugar substitute
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground pepper
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup white vinegar
- 1 large egg
- 1/4 cup fresh or bottled lemon juice
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh chives

## Instructions

1. Boil the potatoes in a large saucepan for about 20 minutes, until tender. Drain, cool slightly, slice, and put in a large serving bowl with the chopped onion.
2. Cook the bacon in a medium skillet until crisp. Drain on paper towels and discard all but 2 tablespoons of the bacon fat.
3. Stir the sugar, flour, salt and pepper into the bacon fat in the skillet.
4. Whisk the water, vinegar, egg and lemon juice together in a small bowl, then whisk into the skillet. Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until it starts to boil. Reduce the heat to low and simmer for 5 minutes.
5. Pour the dressing over the potatoes and onions and toss gently. Garnish with the chives and serve warm.

## Kitchen Notes

The source says Milwaukee has a large German population, German potato salad is common there, and it is often served warm. It is best made the day it is served, and can be reheated in the microwave.
