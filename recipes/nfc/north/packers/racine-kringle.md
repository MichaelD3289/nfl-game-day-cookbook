## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1/2 cup butter
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 pkg. active dry yeast
- 1/4 cup warm water (105 to 115°F)
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup lukewarm milk (scalded, then cooled)

### Pecan filling

- 1 1/2 cups chopped pecans
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 cup butter, softened

### Glaze

- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon water, or more as needed
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Cut the butter into the flour, sugar and salt until the mixture resembles fine crumbs. Dissolve the yeast in the warm water, then stir the yeast, egg and milk into the flour mixture and beat until smooth (the dough will be very soft). Cover and refrigerate at least 2 but no longer than 24 hours.
2. Mix the pecans, brown sugar and softened butter until well combined.
3. Divide the dough in half and return one half to the refrigerator. Roll the other half into a 15-by-6-inch rectangle on a floured surface. Spread half of the filling lengthwise down the center in a 3-inch strip.
4. Fold the sides of the dough over the filling with a 1 1/2-inch overlap and pinch the edges to seal. Arrange on a greased baking sheet in an oval or horseshoe shape, pinching the ends together for the oval. Repeat with the remaining dough and filling.
5. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 30 minutes. Bake at 375°F until golden brown, 20 to 25 minutes.
6. Mix the glaze ingredients until smooth, adding water 1/2 teaspoon at a time if needed. Spread over the kringles and sprinkle with nuts.

## Kitchen Notes

The source says this recipe comes from Racine, Wisconsin, and that its family had kringle for Christmas morning breakfast.
