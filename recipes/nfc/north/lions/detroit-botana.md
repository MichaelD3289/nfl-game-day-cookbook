## Ingredients

- 8 ounces corn chips (one bag)
- 1 can refried pinto beans
- 1/2 pound chorizo (Mexican sausage), chopped
- 1/2 pound Muenster cheese, grated
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 green pepper, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- 10 to 12 olives, sliced
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 2 cups shredded lettuce

## Instructions

1. Mix the beans and chorizo together and fry until the sausage is done.
2. Spread the corn chips evenly on a plate or cookie pan. A pizza pan is ideal.
3. Spread the beans over the chips and sprinkle the grated cheese over them, covering all the beans. Heat in the microwave or oven until the cheese melts.
4. Top with the chopped lettuce first, then sprinkle with more cheese.
5. Arrange the tomatoes, onion, green pepper, avocado and olives over the top. Add Mexican salsa to taste if you like.

## Kitchen Notes

The source says this recipe is from the Mexico Inn in Westland, Michigan, was the most requested recipe on the Friday Feast, and was published in the "Jack McCarthy Restaurant Recipes" cookbook; it adds that the ingredients can be varied to taste. Tostada Magazine says Armando Galan added the botana to the menu at Armando's in Southwest Detroit in 1975.
