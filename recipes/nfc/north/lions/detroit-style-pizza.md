## Ingredients

### Dough

- 3 cups (360 g) bread flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons (9 g) table salt
- 1 teaspoon (3 g) instant yeast
- 1 cup plus 3 tablespoons (270 g) lukewarm water (85–90°F)
- 1 generous tablespoon (15 g) extra-virgin olive oil

### Sauce

- 2 tablespoons (25 g) extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 (28-ounce/794 g) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon (17 g) granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon table salt, plus more to taste

### Assembly

- 1 tablespoon (12 g) extra-virgin olive oil for the pan
- 3 cups (340 g) shredded brick cheese (or 1 1/2 cups/170 g low-moisture mozzarella plus 1 1/2 cups/170 g cheddar)
- 2 ounces (60 g) pepperoni, optional
- 1/2 cup (28 g) finely grated Pecorino Romano
- Red pepper flakes or smoky spicy seasoning, optional

## Instructions

1. Mix flour, salt, and yeast, then add water and oil and stir until combined. Cover and rest 15 minutes; fold the dough several times around the bowl. Rest another 15 minutes, repeat the fold, then cover and rise 1–1 1/2 hours until slightly puffy.
2. For the sauce, cook garlic in olive oil until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add tomatoes, sugar, and salt; simmer about 20 minutes until thickened to roughly 3 cups. Season and cool.
3. Heat the oven to 500°F with a baking steel or stone on a lower rack. Oil a Detroit-style or 9-by-13-inch pan. Transfer dough to the pan, coat with oil, stretch to cover, and rest 45–60 minutes until slightly puffy.
4. Bake the crust 10–12 minutes until set but pale. Make a cheese border around the edge, then add remaining cheese, about 1 1/3 cups sauce in diagonal stripes, and optional pepperoni.
5. Bake 12–14 minutes until edges are dark and crisp. Loosen and remove pizza from pan to a rack; cool briefly, then finish with Pecorino and optional pepper flakes and cut into squares.
6. Let the pan cool just enough to handle, then free the caramelized cheese with a thin spatula and lift the pizza promptly onto a rack to preserve its crisp bottom.
