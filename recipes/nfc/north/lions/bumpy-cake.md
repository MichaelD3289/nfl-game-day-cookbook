## Ingredients

### Cake

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 3/4 cup canola oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup brewed coffee

### Vanilla frosting

- 3/4 cup butter, room temperature
- 5 cups powdered sugar
- 1 to 2 tablespoons milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Chocolate icing

- 3/4 cup butter
- 3/4 cup Dutch process cocoa powder
- 3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 3 tablespoons light corn syrup
- 5 1/2 cups powdered sugar, sifted

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and coat a 9x13-inch baking pan with nonstick spray. In a stand mixer on low, mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder and baking soda.
2. With the mixer on low, add the eggs, buttermilk, oil and vanilla until just combined, then slowly stream in the coffee. Pour into the pan and bake 25 to 30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely on a rack and level the top if it domes.
3. Beat the butter until smooth, then mix in the powdered sugar and 1 tablespoon of the milk for 2 minutes until creamy, adding more milk if stiff. Pipe it in long tubes across the cake, about 1 inch apart, and freeze the cake while you make the icing.
4. Melt the butter, cocoa, cream and corn syrup over medium-low heat, then boil, whisking, for 2 minutes. Whisk in the powdered sugar and cook, whisking, 4 to 5 minutes until smooth and glossy.
5. Cool the icing in a glass bowl for 10 to 15 minutes, whisking often, until warm but not hot.
6. Pour the icing over the frozen cake, coating the vanilla frosting first and then filling in the rest of the top. Let it set before serving.

## Kitchen Notes

Cookies & Cups says Bumpy Cake originated in Detroit and was created by the Sanders Candy Company. Store at room temperature up to 3 days, or freeze airtight up to 2 months.
