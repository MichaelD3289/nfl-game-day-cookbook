## Ingredients

### Brownies

- 5.25 oz 60% dark couverture chocolate
- 9.75 oz butter
- 12.75 oz granulated sugar
- 5 eggs
- 3.5 oz all-purpose flour
- 1/8 teaspoon baking powder
- 8 oz chopped walnuts

### Apricot glaze

- 1 cup water
- 1 cup apricot preserves
- 1 teaspoon unflavored gelatin

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler. Mix the baking powder, sugar and flour together in a bowl. Combine the chocolate and flour mixtures and stir for 4 to 5 minutes. Add the eggs and continue mixing.
3. Pour the mixture into a 9x12 baking sheet. Sprinkle the walnuts on top, pressing down slightly into the mixture with your hand. Bake for approximately 35 minutes, until the edges begin to crisp and the brownies have risen about 1/4 inch.
4. For the glaze, mix the water, apricot preserves and gelatin in a saucepan, stir thoroughly and boil for 2 minutes.
5. Brush the hot glaze over the brownies while they are still warm.

## Kitchen Notes

The Palmer House says the brownie was invented in its kitchen for the 1893 World's Fair. Properly baked, the brownie stays gooey, so a toothpick in the middle may come out moist.
