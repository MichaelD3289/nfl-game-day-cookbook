## Ingredients

### Beef

- 3 pounds boneless beef sirloin or round roast

### Rub

- 1 tablespoon ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper

### Juice

- 6 cups hot water
- 4 beef bouillon cubes

### Sandwiches

- 10 soft, fluffy rolls or Italian bread loaf cut into 10 portions
- 3 green bell peppers
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup spicy hot giardiniera (use the linked Chicago oil-packed giardiniera component) {{component:chicago-oil-packed-giardiniera}}

## Instructions

1. Mix the rub. Lightly wet the roast so the seasoning adheres, then coat generously, reserving leftover rub for the juice. Heat oven to 225°F with a rack just below center.
2. Bring water to a boil in a 9-by-13-inch baking pan and dissolve bouillon. Stir in remaining rub. Set a rack over the pan and place the roast on the rack. Roast until the center reaches 130 to 140°F for medium rare, about 3 hours; time depends on roast shape and oven.
3. While beef roasts, halve peppers, remove stems and seeds, rinse and cut into 1/4-inch strips. Saute in olive oil over medium-high heat until limp and beginning to brown, about 15 minutes; set aside.
4. Remove roast and juice pan. Put roast in a pan and refrigerate until firm, a few hours; chill juice separately. Slice beef against the grain as thinly as possible.
5. Taste juice and thin with water or simmer to concentrate as desired; bring to a gentle simmer. Warm sliced beef in juice for about 1 minute. Do not leave it submerged more than 10 minutes or it can curl and toughen.
6. Cut rolls lengthwise, leaving a hinge. Spoon juice onto bread, add a generous layer of beef, spoon on more juice, then top with peppers and giardiniera. For a wet sandwich, dip the assembled roll in juice; serve with napkins.
