## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef or vegetarian/vegan ground-beef alternative
- 1 onion, small dice
- 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced or minced
- 1 batch homemade hotdish cream sauce (about 3 1/2 cups); or substitute store-bought condensed cream of mushroom soup (two 10–10.5-ounce cans per homemade batch), undiluted {{component:hotdish-cream-sauce}}
- 1 cup frozen corn kernels, thawed
- 1 cup frozen peas, thawed
- 2 cups grated cheddar, divided into two equal portions
- 60–70 frozen unseasoned tater tots (about 1 package; keep frozen until arranging)
- Hot pepper flakes, optional

## Instructions

1. Make the linked cream sauce first if using it. Heat oven to 375°F. Brown the meat and remove it with a slotted spoon, leaving a couple tablespoons of fat in the skillet. Cook onion in the fat for 5–6 minutes, then add garlic and cook 90 seconds. Mix onion and garlic with the meat.
2. Oil or butter a 9-by-13-inch baking dish. Spread the cooked beef-onion mixture in the dish. Pour homemade cream sauce or spoon undiluted mushroom soup over it, scatter corn and peas, then distribute all 2 cups cheddar over the filling. Arrange frozen tater tots in one layer on top; leave them as the exposed top layer.
3. Bake on a rack about 6 inches below the broiler for 45–50 minutes, until bubbling at the sides and tots are browned. Broil briefly only if needed for extra browning, watching closely.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Keep cheese beneath the tots so the frozen potato layer remains the exposed crisp topping. Corn, peas and cheddar are family variations; the familiar beef, condensed mushroom soup and tater-tot layers remain intact.
