## Ingredients

- 6 cups Special K
- 1 1/2 cups peanut butter
- 1 cup corn syrup
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
- 1 cup butterscotch chips

## Instructions

1. Put the cereal in a large heat-proof bowl and set aside.
2. Combine the peanut butter, corn syrup and sugar in a small saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the sugar is dissolved. Pour over the cereal and toss to coat.
3. Spread into a greased 13 x 9-inch pan.
4. Melt the chocolate and butterscotch chips in the microwave, stirring until smooth. Spread over the bars.

## Kitchen Notes

The recipe is credited to a cook from Edgerton, Minnesota. Wikipedia's Scotcheroos article says that in Minnesota, scotcheroos are known as Special K bars.
