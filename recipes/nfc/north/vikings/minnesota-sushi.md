## Ingredients

- 8 oz. (about 8 slices) sliced deli ham
- 8 oz. whipped cream cheese spread (Original, Onion & Chive, Garden Vegetable or Everything Bagel flavor)
- 8 dill pickle spears (or more as needed)

## Instructions

1. Lay the ham on a clean work surface and spread it evenly with the cream cheese spread.
2. Spoon the pickles lengthwise down the center of each slice of ham.
3. Roll the ham lengthwise around the pickles and lay each roll seam-side down.
4. Using a sharp knife, cut the uneven ends off each roll.
5. Cut the rolls into 1/2" slices.

## Kitchen Notes

Kowalski's publishes this under the name "Minnesota Sushi". The source notes the recipe is gluten free when gluten-free deli ham is used.
