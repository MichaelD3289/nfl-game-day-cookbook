## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 pounds 80% lean ground beef (such as chuck)
- 6 slices American cheese
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 onion, diced
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper, quantity to taste
- 6 soft hamburger buns, split

### For serving

- Dill pickle slices

## Instructions

1. Divide beef into 12 portions and form thin patties. Fold each cheese slice into a small square, place on a patty leaving a meat border, cover with another patty, and crimp the edges closed. Repeat to make six stuffed patties.
2. Heat oil in a cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Cook onion until browned, about 10 minutes; transfer it to a bowl and wipe out the pan.
3. Season patties with salt and pepper and cook 7–8 minutes. Flip, pierce the center with a toothpick to release steam, and cook about 7 more minutes for medium-well.
4. Put about three pickle slices on each bun bottom, add onions and a patty, then close with the bun top.
5. Check doneness through the beef away from the cheese pocket; ground beef should reach 160°F (food-safety guidance added by this book; the source does not give a temperature). Rest the burgers a few minutes before eating because the cheese center is extremely hot.
