## Ingredients

### Salad

- 1 1/2 cups uncooked wild rice
- 2 medium apples, chopped
- 2 celery ribs, chopped
- 1/2 cup unblanched almonds, coarsely chopped

### Dressing

- 2 tablespoons plus 1/2 cup orange juice
- 4 teaspoons olive oil
- 4 teaspoons red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the wild rice according to the package directions. Transfer to a large bowl and cool completely.
2. Toss the apples with 2 tablespoons of the orange juice in a small bowl. In another bowl, whisk the remaining orange juice with the olive oil, vinegar, honey, salt and white pepper.
3. Add the apples and celery to the rice. Drizzle with the dressing and toss to combine.
4. Refrigerate, covered, until chilled. Sprinkle with the almonds before serving.

## Kitchen Notes

The recipe is credited to a cook from Elk River, Minnesota, whose note says to use a mixture of red and green apples for color. The source lists halved green grapes, cubed cheddar cheese and chopped red onion as optional toppings; they are not included here.
