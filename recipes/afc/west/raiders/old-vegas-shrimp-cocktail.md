## Ingredients

- 1 pound small cooked salad shrimp, peeled, deveined and tail-off
- 1 cup ketchup
- 1 cup bottled chili sauce, such as Heinz Chili Sauce (not hot chile sauce)
- 2 tablespoons prepared horseradish
- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- ½ teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- Optional 2 drops vinegar-based hot pepper sauce, such as Tabasco

### For serving

- Lemon wedges

## Instructions

1. Chill small tulip, sundae or martini glasses in the freezer for about 20 minutes. Keep the cooked salad shrimp refrigerated while the glasses chill.
2. Whisk ketchup, bottled chili sauce, horseradish, lemon juice and Worcestershire until smooth. Taste; add the two optional drops of vinegar-style hot sauce only if you want more heat.
3. Divide small shrimp among cold glasses. Spoon the sauce over the shrimp to coat; offer any extra sauce on the side. Add lemon wedges and serve immediately.

## Kitchen Notes

Prep: About 10 minutes active plus 20 minutes chilling

Serve in chilled cocktail glasses; the original Golden Gate sauce formula is undisclosed.
