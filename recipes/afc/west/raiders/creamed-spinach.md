## Ingredients

- 2 pounds fresh spinach, tough stems removed and washed
- 3/4 cup heavy cream
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped shallots
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 1/4 cups whole milk
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon grated nutmeg
- 1/4 cup grated Swiss cheese
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spinach for 2 minutes, drain in a fine-mesh strainer and press out as much water as possible. Finely chop and set aside.
2. Bring the cream to a low boil in a small saucepan, then remove from the heat.
3. Melt the butter in a medium heavy saucepan over medium-high heat. Cook the shallots and garlic, stirring, until soft, about 1 minute. Add the flour, then reduce the heat to low and stir constantly until a light blond roux forms, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Whisk in the milk in a steady stream and cook until thick and smooth, 1 to 2 minutes. Add the salt, white pepper and nutmeg and simmer until thickened, 3 to 5 minutes.
5. Whisk in the hot cream and cook for 1 minute. Fold in the Swiss and Parmesan until smooth, then add the spinach, mix well and cook until completely warmed through, 1 to 2 minutes.
6. Adjust the seasoning to taste and serve hot.
