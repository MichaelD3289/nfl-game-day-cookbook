## Ingredients

- One 3-rib standing beef rib roast (7–8 pounds)
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- ¼ cup kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons coarsely ground black or tricolor peppercorns

### For serving

- Optional: about 1 1/2 cups prime rib pan jus, homemade or prepared {{component:prime-rib-pan-jus}}
- Optional: about 1 1/4 cups horseradish cream, homemade or prepared {{component:prime-rib-horseradish-cream}}

## Instructions

1. If making horseradish cream, prepare it now and refrigerate at least 4 hours; this overlaps the roast’s salting time. Pat roast completely dry. With fat side up, make shallow slits in the fat and insert garlic slices. Season all over with measured kosher salt and pepper. Wrap and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
2. Heat oven to 250°F. Unwrap roast and set fat side up on a rack in a roasting pan. Roast until a thermometer in the center, away from bone, reads 120°F, about 4 hours.
3. Remove roast and let stand 1 hour; carryover heat raises its center roughly 10–14°F. Heat oven to 500°F, then return roast for 10–15 minutes until the fat cap is browned and crisp.
4. Rest 30 minutes. During this final rest, prepare the pan jus from the roast drippings if using it. Carve between ribs or cut meat from bones and slice across the grain. Serve as a casino-style carving-station roast with pan juices, horseradish, or au jus if desired (not included in source recipe).

## Kitchen Notes

Prep: 20 minutes active plus 4 hours to overnight salting

Cook: About 4 hours roasting plus 1 hour and 30 minutes resting and 10–15 minutes browning
