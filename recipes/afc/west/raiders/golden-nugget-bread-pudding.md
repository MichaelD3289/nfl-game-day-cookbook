## Ingredients

### Bread pudding

- 4 1/2 slices white bread
- 16 oz half and half
- 8 oz whipping cream
- 8 eggs
- 1 1/4 cups sugar
- 1/4 tsp. vanilla
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2 tbls. raisins

### Bourbon sauce

- 1 lb. brown sugar
- 8 oz. butter
- 1/2 qt. heavy cream
- 1/2 cup bourbon

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Mix the half and half, whipping cream, eggs, 1 1/4 cups sugar and vanilla well.
2. Arrange the bread on the bottom of a 10 x 12 pan and pour the liquid over it.
3. Mix the cinnamon with 1 tsp sugar and sprinkle over the bread. Spread the raisins over the top.
4. Cover with foil and bake in a water bath for 75 to 90 minutes.
5. For the sauce, melt the butter and brown sugar until the sugar is completely dissolved, then turn off the heat. Stir in the heavy cream and bourbon. Serve warm with the pudding.

## Kitchen Notes

The Las Vegas Advisor says the Golden Nugget's bread pudding was renowned during the buffet heyday and was allegedly made from a recipe of Steve Wynn's mother; part of its appeal was the bourbon sauce.
