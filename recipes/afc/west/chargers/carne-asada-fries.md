## Ingredients

### Fries

- 2 russet potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 1/4- to 1/3-inch matchsticks
- Vegetable or peanut oil for frying, enough to fill a large Dutch oven a little less than halfway
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher or sea salt, or to taste

### Carne asada

- 2 pounds beef top sirloin, sliced into 1/2-inch-thick steaks
- 2 teaspoons kosher or sea salt, or to taste
- 1/4 large white onion, for cleaning the grill
- Vegetable oil for greasing the grill, or beef fat trimmed from the meat

### Toppings

- 1 avocado, mashed
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Salsa roja, salsa verde, or both, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven to 325°F. Set a cooling rack over a paper-towel-lined baking sheet.
2. Fry half the dry potatoes for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring so they do not stick, until creamy and matte; remove any that start to brown. Repeat with the rest.
3. Raise the oil to 375–400°F and fry the potatoes in batches for 3 to 4 minutes until golden brown. Drain on the rack, salt right away, and keep warm in a 250°F oven.
4. Heat a grill or grill pan to high, rub it with the onion, and brush with oil or beef fat. Salt the steaks and grill them salted side up 4 to 5 minutes until deeply marked.
5. Flip and cook 3 to 4 minutes more for medium-rare. Rest 5 minutes, then dice into 1/2-inch pieces.
6. Pile the fries on a platter. Spoon the avocado and sour cream around them, top with the beef, sprinkle with the cheddar, and finish with salsa.

## Kitchen Notes

Adapted by Pati Jinich from the carne asada fries at Rolando's Taco Shop in San Diego. The source gives no quantities for the salsa; use your favorite.
