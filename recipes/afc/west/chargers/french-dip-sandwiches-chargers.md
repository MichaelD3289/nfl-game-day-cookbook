## Ingredients

- 2½ pounds trimmed tri-tip roast
- 1½ teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon granulated garlic
- 1 teaspoon granulated onion
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 cups low-sodium beef stock, divided
- ½ cup dry red wine, such as Zinfandel or Syrah (optional; replace with ½ cup beef stock)
- 4 French rolls, split (or 2 baguettes cut into 6-inch lengths)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Rub tri-tip with salt, garlic, onion, pepper and oil. Let stand 1 hour at room temperature while the oven heats to 450°F.
2. Roast 15 minutes at 450°F. Reduce oven to 350°F and roast 10–15 minutes more, until the center reaches 125°F for medium-rare. Rest 15 minutes, then slice very thinly across the grain.
3. For a simplified jus (the source first browns butter and reduces the broth by about a third), simmer wine (if using) in a saucepan until reduced by half. Add stock and simmer 5 minutes; season to taste. For an alcohol-free jus, simmer 2½ cups stock until lightly concentrated.
4. Dip the cut faces of rolls briefly in warm jus, pile with sliced beef and serve each sandwich with a small cup of jus for dipping.
