## Ingredients

### Beer batter

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 cup dark Mexican beer, such as Negra Modelo or Equis XX Amber

### Cream sauce

- 1/3 cup mayonnaise
- 2/3 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons water
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Fish

- 2 pounds skinned halibut, cut into 5 x 1/2-inch strips
- 1 cup all-purpose flour and 1 teaspoon salt, plus more salt and pepper for seasoning
- Oil, for frying, about 1 inch deep in the skillet

### For serving

- Corn tortillas
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 2 cups tomatillo salsa, store-bought or homemade (optional)
- Pickled jalapeños (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix the batter flour, salt and pepper in a medium bowl, gradually whisk in the beer, and let rest 15 minutes.
2. Whisk the lemon zest, lemon juice and water into the mayonnaise and crema; season with salt and pepper. Refrigerate covered up to 3 days.
3. Pour oil about 1 inch deep into a large skillet and heat over medium heat to 350°F.
4. Combine the flour and 1 teaspoon salt on a plate. Season the halibut with salt and pepper, then coat in the seasoned flour.
5. In batches, dip the fish in the beer batter, coating both sides, and fry until golden brown and cooked through, about 5 minutes. Drain on paper towels.
6. Fill corn tortillas with fish and top with cream sauce, cabbage, and, if you like, tomatillo salsa and pickled jalapeños.
