## Ingredients

### Cake

- Nonstick cooking spray, for the cake pan
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting the cake pan
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 4 large eggs, separated
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup whole milk

### Milk soak

- 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
- 1 (12-ounce) can evaporated milk
- 2 cups heavy cream, divided
- 3 tablespoons orange liqueur (Grand Marnier recommended)

### Topping

- 1 tablespoon powdered sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 10-inch cake pan with 2-inch sides, dust with flour, line with parchment and grease the parchment.
2. Whisk the flour and baking powder together.
3. Whip the egg whites until frothy, slowly add the sugar and beat to stiff peaks. Beat in the yolks one at a time, then fold in the flour mixture in three additions, alternating with two additions of the whole milk.
4. Bake about 30 minutes, until a tester comes out clean. Cool about 10 minutes, then turn out onto a platter with 1-inch sides.
5. Poke the warm cake all over with a thick skewer. Stir together the condensed milk, evaporated milk, 1 cup of the cream and the liqueur, pour over the cake, then cover and chill until cold, about 3 hours or overnight.
6. Beat the remaining 1 cup cream with the powdered sugar to soft peaks, spread over the cake and dust with cinnamon.
