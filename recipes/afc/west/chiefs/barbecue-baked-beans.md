## Ingredients

- 1 pound dried navy beans, sorted and rinsed
- About 3 cups water to start simmering beans, plus up to 2 cups more as needed
- ½ cup dark brown sugar
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- ⅓ cup sorghum syrup (traditional molasses-like sweetener; use sorghum for this source recipe)
- ¼ pound chopped smoked beef brisket burnt ends or other barbecue meat scraps
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- ½ teaspoon dry mustard
- Salt, to taste
- Reserved bean-cooking liquid, plus up to 1 cup water to cover for baking

## Instructions

1. Sort and rinse navy beans, removing stones or damaged beans. Put in a large saucepan with about 3 cups water. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently until tender, about 1–1½ hours, adding up to 2 more cups water if needed. Drain and reserve the bean cooking liquid.
2. Heat oven to 275°F. In a bean pot or ovenproof casserole, layer tender beans, dark brown sugar, chopped onion, sorghum and chopped burnt ends or other smoked meat, finishing with beans.
3. Stir in soy sauce, Worcestershire, dry mustard and salt to taste. Add reserved bean liquid, then up to 1 cup fresh water if needed so beans are just covered.
4. Bake uncovered 5–6 hours, stirring occasionally. Cover with a lid or foil if top browns too quickly. Serve hot with barbecue.

## Kitchen Notes

Prep: About 15 minutes active plus 1–1½ hours bean simmer

Start with cooked brisket burnt ends or other prepared smoked barbecue meat.
