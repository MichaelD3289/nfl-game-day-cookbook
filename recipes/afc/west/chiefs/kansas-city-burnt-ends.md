## Ingredients

- 6 pounds USDA Choice or better beef brisket point
- 3 teaspoons Morton coarse kosher salt (about ½ teaspoon per pound)
- ¼ cup Kansas City-style barbecue sauce, preferably a sweet tomato-and-molasses style {{component:kansas-city-barbecue-sauce}}
- ¾ teaspoon brown sugar (equivalent to source’s ¼ tablespoon)
- ¼ cup beef broth, for foil wrap
- About 4 cups hardwood chunks or chips (8 ounces total), for smoker

### Big Bad Beef Rub

- 1½ tablespoons coarse black pepper
- 1½ teaspoons sugar
- 1½ teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon each mustard powder, garlic powder and ancho/chili powder
- ½ teaspoon chipotle or cayenne

## Instructions

1. Trim excess surface fat from the brisket point; save trimmings. Salt the point 12–24 hours ahead (at least 2–4 hours). Mix the listed spice-rub ingredients and apply about ¼ cup; keep meat chilled. If instead using a presalted bottled rub, omit the separate salt.
2. Stabilize a smoker or indirect grill near 225–235°F. Add half the wood when the cold meat goes on, then the remaining wood during the first 2 hours. Smoke, turning after about 3 hours only if browning unevenly, until the point has dark bark and reaches about 155°F internally.
3. Wrap tightly in two layers of heavy foil with ¼ cup beef broth. Return to indirect heat until the center reaches about 195°F; reserve foil juices. Allow roughly 10 hours total cooking, guided by temperature and tenderness rather than the clock.
4. Cut the point into approximately 1-inch cubes. Dust lightly with any reserved rub and the brown sugar. Briefly brown the cubes over direct heat on a grill topper.
5. Render reserved brisket fat in a skillet; crisp the cubes in it, turning. Drain excess fat. Add ¼ cup Kansas City-style sauce plus about ¼ cup reserved foil juices (if available). Return to the grill, stirring every 5 minutes until glaze clings and the cubes sizzle. Serve while bark is crisp.

## Kitchen Notes

Prep: 10 minutes active plus 12–24 hours advance salting

Use a smoker or indirect grill; brisket size and heat control affect the finish time.
