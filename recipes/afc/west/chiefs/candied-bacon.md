## Ingredients

- 12 oz thick cut bacon
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp. your favorite BBQ rub

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a sheet pan with foil and set a wire rack on it.
2. Lay the bacon strips on the rack. Coat one side of each strip in brown sugar, then sprinkle with the BBQ rub.
3. Bake for 10 minutes.
4. Turn the oven off and leave the bacon inside with the door closed. After 5 minutes, check it.
5. Once the tips start to turn dark, remove the rack from the oven. Let the bacon cool, then serve.
