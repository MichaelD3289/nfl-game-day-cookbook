## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon canola or vegetable oil
- 4 ounces smoked ham steak, diced ½ inch
- 6 fresh sweet-corn ears, husked (or two 10-ounce bags frozen corn per source)
- 1 cup whole milk
- 6 ounces full-fat block cream cheese, softened and diced
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 1 teaspoon Diamond Crystal kosher salt (or ½ teaspoon table salt)
- ½ teaspoon ground cayenne
- 3 ounces extra-sharp cheddar, freshly shredded
- 3 ounces naturally smoked cheddar, freshly shredded (avoid artificial smoke flavor)
- Optional thinly sliced scallion greens

## Instructions

1. Brown diced smoked ham in oil in a large skillet; transfer to a plate. Cut kernels off the cobs. Scrape the cobs over a fine strainer to collect starchy corn milk; omit this scraping step if using frozen corn.
2. Add corn kernels and corn milk to skillet with ham, whole milk, cream cheese, garlic, measured salt and cayenne. Cook gently, stirring, until cream cheese melts and small bubbles appear at the edge.
3. Lower heat and stir in both freshly grated cheddars until just melted and glossy. Do not boil after cheese goes in. Top with scallion greens if desired.
