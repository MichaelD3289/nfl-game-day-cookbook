## Ingredients

- 12 large eggs
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, divided
- 1/2 green bell pepper, cut into 1/2-inch dice
- 1/2 red bell pepper, cut into 1/2-inch dice
- 6 ounces boiled ham (in one piece), cut into 1/2-inch dice
- 1/2 cup chopped white onion (editor's classic Denver substitution for scallions)
- Optional: 1/2 cup grated Monterey Jack, cheddar or Gouda (about 2 ounces)

## Instructions

1. Melt 3 tablespoons butter in a small covered nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add white onion and cook 2 minutes. Add peppers, cover and cook without stirring until tender, about 3 minutes.
2. Uncover and raise heat to medium-high. Add ham and cook, stirring frequently, until hot, about 1 minute. Transfer filling to a bowl.
3. For each omelet, beat 3 eggs and season lightly with salt and pepper. Melt a small piece of remaining butter in the skillet over medium heat. Add one-quarter of the filling, pour in eggs and stir constantly with a heat-resistant spatula until just cooked, about 30 seconds.
4. Turn off heat and smooth eggs evenly in pan. If using cheese, sprinkle about 2 tablespoons over omelet. Rest 30 seconds until set, fold in half and transfer to a warm plate. Cover with foil and repeat to make four omelets.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: The Food Network identifies Western omelette as another name for Denver omelette, describes ham, bell peppers and onions as core ingredients, and explicitly suggests 1/2 cup chopped white onion cooked 2 minutes before peppers in place of scallions. Its base recipe is 15 minutes prep and 15 minutes cook for four servings. Cheese remains optional; source notes it is not traditional.
