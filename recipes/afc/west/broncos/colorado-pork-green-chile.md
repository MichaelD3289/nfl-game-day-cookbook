## Ingredients

- 2 pounds roasted, peeled and chopped Anaheim or Big Jim green chiles (weigh prepared; raw chiles will weigh more)
- 2 pounds pork shoulder, trimmed and cut in 1-inch cubes
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 medium yellow onions, coarsely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 pound fresh tomatillos, husked and diced (canned substitute allowed by source)
- 1½ teaspoons dried Mexican oregano
- ½ teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 pound very ripe tomatoes, diced (canned substitute allowed)
- 4 cups chicken stock
- 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- Optional cayenne pepper, to taste

### For serving

- Warm flour tortillas, quantity to appetite

## Instructions

1. If starting with raw chiles, roast at 450°F 20–25 minutes until blistered. Steam in a closed bag 20–30 minutes, then peel, seed and chop; measure 2 pounds prepared chiles.
2. Season pork with salt and pepper. Brown in batches in the vegetable oil in a heavy pot; remove pork and reserve 3–4 tablespoons of pan fat if available.
3. Cook diced onions, minced garlic and tomatillos in about 2 tablespoons of reserved fat until softened. Return pork to pot with prepared green chiles, Mexican oregano, cumin, tomatoes and stock. Simmer gently 1–2 hours until pork is fork-tender.
4. Add cubed russet potatoes about 45 minutes before the intended finish and cook until tender. Taste and adjust salt, pepper and optional cayenne. Serve with warm flour tortillas.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Potatoes cook during final 45 minutes; browning in batches adds time.
