## Ingredients

### Dry mix

- 1 1/4 cups cornmeal
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1/4 cup packed brown sugar

### Wet mix

- 1 stick salted butter, at room temperature
- 1/2 cup Mexican crema
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup roasted, peeled, diced Pueblo chiles
- 1 cup Colorado corn kernels
- 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar

## Instructions

1. Grill and roast the chiles, then cover them in a bowl with plastic wrap to steam off the charred skin. Peel and dice.
2. Heat the oven to 400°F. Line a 10-inch cast iron skillet with parchment and lightly spray it.
3. Whisk the dry mix together. In another bowl, blend the butter and crema, then add the milk, eggs, chiles, corn and cheese and let rest about 5 minutes.
4. Fold half the wet mixture into the dry, then the rest, until just combined.
5. Spread in the skillet and bake 35 to 45 minutes, checking at 35 with a toothpick.
6. Cool at least 10 minutes before serving.

## Kitchen Notes

Colorado Proud's recipe of the month pairs roasted Pueblo green chile with corn and cheese as a side for fall meals.
