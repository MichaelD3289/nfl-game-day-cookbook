## Ingredients

- 2 pounds calf testicles
- 2 cups beer
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup yellow cornmeal
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 tablespoon hot pepper sauce
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- Cocktail sauce or horseradish sauce, for serving

## Instructions

1. Split the tough membrane around each testicle with a sharp knife and peel off the skin, which is easiest while the meat is thawing. Leave whole or slice into 1/4- to 1/2-inch-thick ovals.
2. Cover the pieces with the beer in a large pan or bowl and soak at least 2 hours; longer is better.
3. In a shallow bowl, combine the eggs, flour, cornmeal, hot pepper sauce, salt and pepper. Drain the pieces and coat them thoroughly in the mixture.
4. Heat oil in a large, deep pot to 375°F. Fry about 3 minutes, until golden brown; they float when done.
5. Drain on paper towels and serve warm with cocktail or horseradish sauce.

## Kitchen Notes

The source ties the dish to Colorado's open-range cattle industry, when calves were castrated at round-ups and the testicles cooked as a treat. The source lists the hot pepper sauce without saying where it goes; this recipe adds it to the coating.
