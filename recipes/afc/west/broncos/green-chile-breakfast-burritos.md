## Ingredients

- 8 ounces sliced bacon, chopped
- 1½ pounds Yukon Gold or red-skinned potatoes, cut in 1-inch cubes (or 3 cups frozen cubed hash browns)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- ⅛ cup canola or vegetable oil
- 1 teaspoon Southwest spice blend or New Mexico red chile powder
- 1 medium sweet or yellow onion, chopped
- 4 small garlic cloves, chopped
- 5 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 5–6 warm 8–10-inch flour tortillas
- 3–4 cups prepared Colorado-style pork green chile, warmed (use the booklet’s Colorado pork green chile recipe)
- 2 cups freshly grated cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Cut bacon into small pieces and fry until crisp. Drain and set aside. Cut potatoes into 1-inch cubes; simmer in water about 5 minutes until partly tender, then drain and pat dry. If using frozen cubed hash browns, cook by package directions instead.
2. Heat canola oil in the bacon skillet over medium heat. Add potatoes, salt and the Southwest spice blend or red chile powder. Fry, stirring, 5–10 minutes until browned at the edges. Add onion for last 3 minutes, then garlic for 30 seconds. Lightly mash a few potatoes to bind the filling.
3. Beat eggs lightly, pour over hot potatoes, and stir just until eggs are softly set; they finish in the oven. Heat oven to 350°F and warm the flour tortillas.
4. Divide potato-egg filling among 5–6 tortillas, add crisp bacon, roll and place seam down on oven-safe plates or in a baking dish. Spoon ½–1 cup warm Colorado green chile over each burrito, dividing 3–4 cups total. Top with grated cheddar. Bake 5–10 minutes until cheese melts; serve immediately.

## Kitchen Notes

Make the Colorado pork green chile ahead; its time is separate from burrito assembly.
