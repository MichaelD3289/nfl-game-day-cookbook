## Ingredients

- 1 cup Bisquick mix
- 1 cup milk
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 5 to 6 ripe Palisade peaches, sliced
- 1 cup sugar
- Vanilla ice cream, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. In an ungreased 8 x 8-inch baking dish, mix the Bisquick, milk, nutmeg and cinnamon until smooth. Stir in the melted butter.
3. Toss the sliced peaches with the sugar in a separate bowl.
4. Spoon the peaches over the batter.
5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes, until golden brown.
6. Serve with vanilla ice cream.

## Kitchen Notes

Palisade peaches grow around Palisade on Colorado's Western Slope, and the source credits their sweet juice and buttery texture.
