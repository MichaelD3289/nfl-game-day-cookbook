## Ingredients

- 3 strips thick-cut bacon
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 1 celery rib, finely diced (optional)
- 1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme (optional)
- 2 bay leaves (optional)
- 2 medium white potatoes, peeled and diced
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups bottled clam juice
- 1 pound chopped hard-shell clam meat, such as quahogs with juices
- Kosher salt to taste
- 3 cups light cream (18–20% milk fat; half-and-half is a lighter substitute)
- 1 teaspoon white pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook bacon in a large pot over medium-low heat until crisp, 10 to 12 minutes. Remove, crumble and retain the fat.
2. Add butter, onion, celery, thyme and bay leaves to the pot. Cook until onion softens, 6 to 8 minutes, then return bacon.
3. Boil diced potatoes separately in salted water until tender, 5 to 8 minutes; drain.
4. Stir flour into the bacon-vegetable mixture for 3 minutes. Gradually stir in clam juice one cup at a time.
5. Add potatoes and clam meat with juices; stir over medium-high heat about 5 minutes until clams are tender.
6. Lower heat. Stir in light cream and white pepper; warm gently without a hard boil so cream stays smooth. Remove bay leaves, taste, and add salt only as needed because clam juice is salty.
