## Ingredients

- Vegetable oil, for frying (enough to half-fill a 3- or 4-quart pot)
- 2 cups corn flour
- 1 teaspoon fine kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 can (5 ounces) evaporated milk
- 1 pound shucked whole-belly steamer clams
- Lemon juice, for serving
- Tartar sauce, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180°F and line a rimmed baking sheet with paper towels.
2. Fill a 3- or 4-quart pot about halfway with oil and heat over medium heat to 350–375°F, checking with an instant-read thermometer.
3. Whisk the corn flour, salt, black pepper and cayenne in a medium bowl. Pour the evaporated milk into a second bowl.
4. Toss a handful of clams in the evaporated milk, then in the corn flour mixture until fully coated. Repeat in batches.
5. Fry the clams 1 to 2 minutes, until golden and the loud sizzling dies down, adjusting the heat to hold the oil in range.
6. Lift out with a slotted spoon onto the lined sheet and keep warm in the oven while frying the rest. Serve hot with lemon juice and tartar sauce.

## Kitchen Notes

Steve Woodman, who runs Woodman's of Essex on Boston's North Shore, swears by this breading: the corn flour adds crunch and the evaporated milk adds sweetness. Shucked clams are pricey, so save these for a special game day.
