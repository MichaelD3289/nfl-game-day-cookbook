## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, softened
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 medium celery stalks, finely chopped (about 1/2 cup)
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh chives (optional)
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1 pound cooked Maine lobster meat, fresh or thawed
- 4 New England split-top hot dog buns
- 4 Bibb lettuce leaves (optional)

### For serving

- Lemon wedges

## Instructions

1. Stir mayonnaise, finely chopped celery, chives if using, lemon juice, and 1/4 teaspoon salt in a bowl.
2. Pat cooked lobster dry, cut into bite-size pieces, and fold gently into dressing. Taste and add a little more salt or lemon only if needed; chill while toasting buns.
3. Spread the outer cut faces of split-top buns with the 2 tablespoons softened butter. Toast in a skillet over medium heat about 3 minutes per side until golden.
4. Open buns slightly, add Bibb lettuce if desired, divide cold lobster salad evenly among four rolls, and serve with lemon wedges.
