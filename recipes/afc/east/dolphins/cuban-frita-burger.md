## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground chuck
- 3 tablespoons Spanish paprika
- 2 teaspoons granulated garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin
- 1 tablespoon Crystal Louisiana hot sauce (or another vinegar-forward cayenne sauce)
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 2 medium russet potatoes
- About 1 cup cooking oil for the potatoes
- 5 soft Cuban-style or hamburger rolls
- Salt, to taste
- Ketchup, optional

## Instructions

1. Shred potatoes, rinse until water runs clear, and squeeze them dry. Fry in hot oil until crisp; drain and salt.
2. Mix ground beef with paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cumin and hot sauce. Form five balls.
3. Heat a skillet to medium-high. Smash seasoned beef balls into thin patties, add diced onion and salt, cook 45–60 seconds, then flip and cook until browned and beef reaches 160°F.
4. Warm the buns against the patties or toast separately. Assemble with meat, onion and crisp potatoes; add ketchup if desired.
