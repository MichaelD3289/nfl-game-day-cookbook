## Ingredients

### Batter

- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour, sifted
- 2 tablespoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 tablespoon mashed roasted garlic, or 1/2 tablespoon minced raw garlic
- 2 tablespoons finely diced sweet onion
- 1 tablespoon seeded, finely diced jalapeño
- 2 tablespoons seeded, finely diced red bell pepper
- 1/4 cup beer
- 4 squirts Tabasco, or to taste

### Fritters

- 3/4 pound cleaned conch meat, freshly ground
- Canola or peanut oil, for deep-frying
- Kosher salt, Tabasco and lemon or lime wedges, to finish

## Instructions

1. Whisk the egg and milk together. In a separate bowl, sift the flour, baking powder, salt and pepper, then stir in the egg mixture.
2. Stir in the garlic, onion, jalapeño, red bell pepper, beer and Tabasco. Fold in the ground conch, cover and refrigerate 30 minutes.
3. Heat the oil to 350°F.
4. Drop small balls of batter into the oil with two spoons or a small scoop. Fry, turning occasionally, until golden, about 5 minutes per batch.
5. Drain on paper towels, season with kosher salt, Tabasco and lemon or lime, and serve hot.

## Kitchen Notes

Norman Van Aken, known as the founding father of New World Cuisine, calls these some of the first flavors of Key West he came to know and love. He uses cleaned conch meat from the Turks and Caicos.
