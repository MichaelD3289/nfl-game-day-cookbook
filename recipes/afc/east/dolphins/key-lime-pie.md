## Ingredients

### Graham cracker crust

- 1 paper-wrapped package graham crackers (1/3 of a 1-pound box), or 1 cup plus 2 1/2 tablespoons graham cracker crumbs
- 5 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1/3 cup sugar

### Filling

- 3 egg yolks
- 1 1/2 teaspoons grated lime zest (from 2 limes)
- 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
- 2/3 cup freshly squeezed Key lime juice, or regular lime juice

### Topping

- 1 cup heavy or whipping cream, chilled
- 3 tablespoons confectioners' sugar

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9-inch pie pan. Process the graham crackers to crumbs, or crush them in a sealed bag with a rolling pin. Mix in the butter and sugar.
2. Press the crumbs into the bottom and sides of the pan with a neat border. Bake until set and golden, 8 minutes, and cool on a rack. Leave the oven on.
3. Beat the egg yolks and zest on high speed until very fluffy, about 5 minutes. Gradually add the condensed milk and beat until thick, 3 or 4 minutes more. On low speed, slowly add the lime juice, mixing just until combined.
4. Pour into the crust and bake until set, about 10 minutes. Cool on a rack, then refrigerate. Freeze for 15 to 20 minutes before serving.
5. Whip the cream with the confectioners' sugar until nearly stiff. Cut the pie into wedges and serve very cold, each with a large dollop of whipped cream.

## Kitchen Notes

This is the pie served at Joe's Stone Crab in Miami Beach. Use Key limes if you can get them; regular limes work too.
