## Ingredients

### Beef and jus

- 2 teaspoons kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 2 teaspoons black pepper, plus more to taste
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 3/4 teaspoon celery seeds
- 3/4 teaspoon caraway seeds
- 1 beef eye-of-round roast (2 pounds)
- 4 tablespoons canola or other neutral oil, divided
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 3 large garlic cloves
- 1 1/2 cups beef broth

### Horseradish cream

- 2 garlic cloves
- 2/3 cup mayonnaise
- 2/3 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup prepared horseradish
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

### Rolls

- 5 to 6 kummelweck rolls (or plain Kaiser rolls to finish with caraway and salt)
- 1 teaspoon caraway seeds
- 3/4 teaspoon flaky salt
- 1 large egg white

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 400°F. Mix the salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, celery seeds and caraway seeds; rub the roast with 1 tablespoon oil and coat with seasoning.
2. Sear roast in 2 tablespoons oil in a Dutch oven, about 6 minutes total. Cover, transfer to the 400°F oven, immediately lower oven to 325°F, and cook about 20–30 minutes until the center reaches 125°F for rare. Rest 20 minutes before slicing.
3. For the cream, mince 2 garlic cloves and combine with mayonnaise, sour cream, horseradish and salt.
4. For jus, sauté sliced onion and remaining 3 sliced garlic cloves in 1 tablespoon oil in the roast pot, about 5 minutes. Add broth, scrape up browned bits and simmer 5 minutes. Add juices from the rested roast; season.
5. Split rolls, brush with egg white, sprinkle caraway and flaky salt, and toast about 5 minutes.
6. Slice beef thinly across the grain. Spread horseradish cream on rolls, dip beef in jus, add beef and optional cooked onions, and serve with remaining jus.
