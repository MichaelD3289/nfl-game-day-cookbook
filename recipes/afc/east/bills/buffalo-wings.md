## Ingredients

- 5 pounds chicken wings (about 30 whole wings), split at the joints with tips removed
- Peanut or canola oil, 2 inches deep in a heavy pot (about 2–3 quarts; pot size varies)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### Buffalo wing sauce

- 1 cup Frank's RedHot Original Cayenne Pepper Sauce (plain hot sauce) {{component:buffalo-wing-sauce}}
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted

### Blue-cheese dip

- 1 cup mayonnaise {{component:blue-cheese-dip}}
- ½ cup sour cream
- ¼ cup crumbled blue cheese
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### For serving

- Celery sticks

## Instructions

1. Make the dip first: stir mayonnaise, sour cream, blue cheese, lemon juice and garlic together. Season with salt and pepper, cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour.
2. Pour oil into a Dutch oven or other heavy pot to a depth of 2 inches. Clip on a deep-fry thermometer and heat to 350°F. Set a wire rack over a rimmed tray or line a tray with paper towels.
3. Pat the wings dry. Fry in uncrowded batches at 350°F for 12–15 minutes, turning once or twice, until golden, crisp and cooked through (165°F at the thickest part). Let the oil return to 350°F between batches. Drain each batch and season lightly with salt and pepper.
4. Whisk the plain cayenne hot sauce with melted butter and toss the fried wings while hot. Serve immediately with celery and the chilled homemade dip. For a quick sauce option, use Anchor Bar Original or Frank’s prepared Buffalo Wings Sauce without adding butter; a baking option is a home variation, not the original fried method.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Anchor Bar-attributed method: unbreaded wings are deep-fried, then tossed in plain cayenne hot sauce and butter. The dip makes about 2 cups and needs 1 hour chilling. Batch time depends on pot size; fry to 165°F. The Make It or Buy It sauce and dip use separate homemade batch sizes.
