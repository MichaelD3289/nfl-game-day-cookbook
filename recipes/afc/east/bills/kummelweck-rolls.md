## Ingredients

- 2 1/2 teaspoons active dry or instant yeast
- 1 cup warm water (95 to 110°F)
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon barley malt syrup or honey
- 2 large egg whites
- 3 cups (13 1/2 ounces) bread flour or high-gluten flour
- 1 tablespoon water, for the egg wash
- Coarse sea salt or kosher salt, for topping
- Caraway seeds, for topping

## Instructions

1. Stir 1/4 cup of the warm water with the yeast and let it rest about 5 minutes. Combine the remaining water, the oil, sugar, salt, malt syrup or honey and 1 egg white.
2. Add half the flour and the yeast mixture and stir with a spoon, then add the remaining flour. Knead 5 to 7 minutes until smooth, elastic and tacky but not sticky.
3. Let the dough rise in an oiled, covered bowl about 1 hour, until doubled. Deflate it, cover and let it rise 30 minutes more.
4. Divide into 8 pieces, shape into balls and flatten slightly. Place on a lined or greased sheet, spray lightly with oil, cover and rise 30 minutes while the oven heats to 425°F.
5. Whisk the second egg white with the water and brush over the rolls. Cut a crescent-shaped slit in each, sprinkle with coarse salt and caraway seeds, and mist with water.
6. Bake 5 minutes, mist again quickly, then bake about 20 minutes more, until browned. Cool on a wire rack.

## Kitchen Notes

Kummelweck rolls are a staple in Western New York, especially around Buffalo and Rochester, where they hold thinly sliced roast beef with horseradish and au jus for beef on weck. Think of a kaiser roll topped with coarse salt and caraway.
