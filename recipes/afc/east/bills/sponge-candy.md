## Ingredients

- Butter for greasing the pan
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup light corn syrup
- 1/8 teaspoon sea salt
- 3 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 12 ounces dark chocolate (about 60–70% cacao; milk chocolate if preferred), chopped

## Instructions

1. Butter an 8-inch square pan, line with overhanging parchment, and butter the paper.
2. In a deep saucepan, heat sugar, butter, light corn syrup and salt over medium-high heat. Stir only until sugar dissolves, then swirl pan until amber and a candy thermometer reads 300°F, about 10–15 minutes.
3. Remove from heat and promptly mix in baking soda and vanilla; it will expand. Pour into the pan without disturbing it and let set for 1 to 2 hours.
4. Break into 1- to 2-inch pieces. Melt chocolate, dip each piece, and let the coating set.
