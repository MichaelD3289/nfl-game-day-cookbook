## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded mozzarella

### Fries

- 8 large Russet potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch-thick wedges
- Neutral oil, such as canola or vegetable, for deep-frying (5 inches deep in the pot)
- Kosher salt

### Gravy

- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1 shallot, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 2 cups chicken stock
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Soak the potato wedges in ice water 30 to 60 minutes, drain, and pat dry in batches. Let them dry another 30 minutes.
2. Heat the oil to 315°F. Fry in batches about 4 minutes until pale and slightly limp, not browned. Drain on a wire rack, reheating the oil between batches, then cool 2 to 4 hours.
3. For the gravy, melt 2 tablespoons butter and cook the shallot until translucent. Add the garlic for 1 to 2 minutes, season with salt and pepper, then add the remaining butter and stir in the flour until lightly browned.
4. Whisk in the stock and Worcestershire until smooth. Simmer about 5 minutes until it coats a spoon, then keep warm on low, stirring often.
5. Reheat the oil to 350°F and fry the potatoes in batches 2 to 3 minutes until crisp and golden. Season with salt and keep warm in a 200°F oven if needed. Preheat the broiler.
6. Arrange the fries on a baking sheet or platter, pour on the gravy, top with the mozzarella, and broil about 1 minute until melted.
