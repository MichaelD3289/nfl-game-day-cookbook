## Ingredients

- 5 kirby cucumbers, or as many as fit in the jar
- 1/2 teaspoon coriander seeds, plus extra for the top
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns, plus extra for the top
- 3 bay leaves
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- A few sprigs fresh dill
- 1/4 cup sea salt
- 6 cups water
- 1/4 teaspoon Ball Pickle Crisp granules (optional)

## Instructions

1. Wash the cucumbers. Dissolve the salt in the water.
2. Crush the coriander, mustard seeds, peppercorns and 2 of the bay leaves with a mortar and pestle, or in a plastic bag with a rolling pin.
3. Pack the cucumbers into a half-gallon jar. Add the garlic and crushed spices, then pour in the salt water, discarding any left over.
4. Add the dill, the remaining bay leaf, a few extra coriander seeds and peppercorns, and the Pickle Crisp if using.
5. Make sure the cucumbers are fully submerged, close the jar and shake. Refrigerate at least 4 days before eating.

## Kitchen Notes

The salt-to-water ratio is set for a half-gallon jar, so use that size. The pickles keep for several weeks in the refrigerator.
