## Ingredients

- Semolina or cornmeal for dusting
- 1 cup prepared sauce, divided
- 7 ounces shredded whole-milk, low-moisture mozzarella
- 1/4 cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
- Red pepper flakes and dried oregano, optional

### Dough

- 3 cups bread flour
- Scant 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2 teaspoons table salt
- 1/4 teaspoon instant yeast
- Scant 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 3/4 cup plus 2 1/2 tablespoons cool water

### Sauce

- 1 can (28 ounces) whole peeled canned tomatoes, preferably plum
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon sugar

## Instructions

1. Mix dough ingredients, knead to a rough cohesive mass, cover and rest 1 hour. Divide in two, shape tight balls, and rest covered 8 to 10 hours.
2. Set a baking steel or stone in the oven and preheat to 500°F for at least 1 hour.
3. Drain and coarsely blend tomatoes; season with salt and sugar.
4. Stretch each dough ball to about 13 inches. Put on a semolina-dusted peel, top with about 1/2 cup sauce, half the mozzarella and half the Parmesan.
5. Bake on the steel or stone about 5 to 7 minutes until the bottom colors; move to upper rack and broil 2 to 3 minutes until spotted and browned.
6. Cool briefly on a rack, finish with optional pepper flakes and oregano, and repeat with second pizza.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Source total about 12 hours includes 8–10-hour dough rest and oven preheat.
