## Ingredients

### Sponge cake crust

- 1/3 cup cake flour, sifted
- 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 pinch salt
- 2 extra-large eggs, separated
- 1/3 cup sugar, divided
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 2 drops pure lemon extract
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- 1/4 teaspoon cream of tartar

### Filling

- 32 ounces full-fat cream cheese (four 8-ounce packages), at room temperature
- 1 2/3 cups sugar
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
- 2 extra-large eggs
- 3/4 cup heavy or whipping cream

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-inch springform pan well and wrap the outside in foil. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt together.
2. Beat the yolks on high for about 3 minutes, slowly add 2 tablespoons of the sugar, and beat until thick and pale yellow, about 5 minutes more. Mix in the vanilla and lemon extracts, fold in the dry ingredients, then stir in the butter.
3. In a clean bowl, whip the whites with the cream of tartar until frothy, add the remaining sugar gradually and beat to stiff, glossy peaks. Fold in a third of the whites, then the rest. Spread in the pan and bake about 10 minutes, until the center springs back. Cool in the pan and leave the oven on.
4. Beat one package of cream cheese with 1/3 cup of the sugar and the cornstarch on low for about 3 minutes. Add the remaining cream cheese one package at a time, then the rest of the sugar and the vanilla on medium. Mix in the eggs one at a time, then the cream briefly. Pour over the crust.
5. Set the pan in a larger pan of hot water reaching about 1 inch up its sides. Bake about 1 1/4 hours, until the edges are light golden and the top slightly tan.
6. Lift the pan out of the water and cool on a rack for 2 hours. Cover loosely and refrigerate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight. Remove the pan sides and slice with a sharp, straight-edged knife.

## Kitchen Notes

Junior's, which started in downtown Brooklyn, bakes its cheesecake on a fluffy sponge cake crust. Refrigerate leftovers, or wrap and freeze them for up to 1 month.
