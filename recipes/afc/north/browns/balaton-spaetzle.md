## Ingredients

- 2 pounds all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1 dozen eggs, beaten
- 2 quarts water, for boiling {{no-scale}}
- Olive oil, to finish

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, salt and eggs together into a batter.
2. Bring the water to a boil.
3. Drop the batter into the boiling water by the tablespoon or through a spaetzle maker.
4. When the dumplings rise to the top, lift them out with a strainer.
5. Serve with a touch of olive oil. At Balaton, the spaetzle goes on the plate first and the goulash on top.

## Kitchen Notes

Balaton has served Hungarian food in Cleveland since 1964, starting in the Buckeye neighborhood. Chef and co-owner Krisztina Ponti, a native of Hungary, shared this spaetzle with Cleveland Magazine along with her goulash.
