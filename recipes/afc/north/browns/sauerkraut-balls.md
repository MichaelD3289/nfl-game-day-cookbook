## Ingredients

### Filling

- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup minced speck (smoked prosciutto)
- 3 tablespoons rye flour
- 1 cup drained sauerkraut
- 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

### Breading and frying

- 6 eggs
- 1 cup water
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 cups crushed panko
- 1 quart rendered animal fat, suet or lard, or vegetable or canola oil, for frying {{no-scale}}

### To serve

- Cocktail sauce, Dijon mustard and mayonnaise

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large sauté pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and garlic until soft, about 4 minutes, then add the speck and cook 2 minutes. Stir in the rye flour for 3 minutes.
2. Add the sauerkraut, vinegar and dill and cook until thickened, about 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper, spread on a sheet, and refrigerate uncovered for at least 2 hours or overnight.
3. Heat the fat in a Dutch oven to 350°F. Whisk the eggs with the water in a deep bowl and set the flour and panko in two shallow bowls.
4. Roll the chilled filling into ping-pong-sized balls. Coat each in flour, dip in the egg mixture, then coat fully in panko.
5. Fry a few at a time until golden brown, about 4 minutes. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with cocktail sauce, mustard and mayonnaise.

## Kitchen Notes

Sauerkraut balls hail from Akron. Jonathon Sawyer, a Cleveland chef, says any restaurant serving Eastern European food in the Cleveland and Akron area has them front and center.
