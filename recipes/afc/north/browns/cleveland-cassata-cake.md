## Ingredients

### Custard

- 2 cups half and half, or 1 cup milk plus 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 6 egg yolks
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
- 6 tablespoons butter, sliced

### Sponge cake

- 8 egg whites, at room temperature
- 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 1/4 cups cake flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 5 egg yolks
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1/2 cup canola oil
- 1/2 cup water

### Strawberries and syrup

- 16 ounces strawberries, chopped, a few saved whole
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 cup Grand Marnier

### Whipped cream

- 4 cups whipping cream
- 3 cups confectioners' sugar

## Instructions

1. Heat the vanilla and half and half until lightly simmering. Whisk the sugar, yolks, cornstarch and salt into a paste, temper with a little hot liquid, return to the pan and simmer 5 to 8 minutes until thick. Off the heat whisk in the butter, press plastic wrap on the surface, cool, then chill overnight.
2. Heat the oven to 325°F and line two greased 9 by 9 inch pans with greased parchment. Whip the egg whites and cream of tartar until foamy, add the sugar gradually and beat to stiff peaks. Sift the flour, baking powder and salt together. Whisk the yolks, lemon juice, water and oil for 2 to 3 minutes until pale, stir into the dry ingredients, then fold in the whites.
3. Fill the pans about two-thirds full and bake 30 to 35 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in the pans for an hour, turn out, cool fully, then return to the pans, cover and chill overnight.
4. Set the chopped strawberries in a sieve over a bowl, stir in the sugar and drain for about an hour. Whisk the drained syrup with the Grand Marnier. Chill the syrup and the strawberries overnight.
5. Whip the cream and confectioners' sugar for about 10 minutes until thick. Slice each cake in half to make four layers. Brush each layer with syrup, spread strawberries and pipe custard, then stack.
6. Coat the cake with a thin layer of whipped cream, freeze 20 minutes, then cover with the remaining cream. Decorate with the reserved strawberries and chill until serving, up to 24 hours.

## Kitchen Notes

Based on the famous cassata cake from Corbo's Bakery. Make the custard, cakes and syrup the day before you assemble; the custard and cakes keep up to two days.
