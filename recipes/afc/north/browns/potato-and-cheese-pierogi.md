## Ingredients

### Dough

- 3 1/2 cups flour
- Pinch of salt
- 2 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon sour cream
- 1 cup lukewarm water

### Filling

- 5 pounds starchy Idaho russet potatoes
- 1/4 pound Velveeta, cubed
- 1/4 pound American cheese, cubed
- Pinch of salt
- Dash of white pepper

### For serving

- Melted butter

## Instructions

1. Whisk water, sour cream and eggs. Mix into flour and salt, then knead just until smooth. Divide, cover, and rest 10 minutes so dough rolls without springing back.
2. Boil peeled, cubed russets until fork-tender; drain thoroughly. Mash with cubed Velveeta and American cheese, salt and white pepper, then cool fully. Roll rested dough 1/8 inch thick and cut 3-inch rounds.
3. Put a scoop of filling on each dough round, fold over, and pinch edges closed firmly.
4. Boil pierogi in salted water without overcrowding. Stir gently; once they float, cook 3 minutes more, then drain and toss with melted butter.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Source gives 10-minute dough rest, then boil potatoes until tender and pierogi until floating plus 3 minutes; batch count and hand shaping vary.
