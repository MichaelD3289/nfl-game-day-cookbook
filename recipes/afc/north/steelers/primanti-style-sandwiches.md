## Ingredients

- 3 cups finely shredded Savoy cabbage
- 1 tablespoon cider vinegar
- 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed celery seed
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to season
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 2 small russet potatoes
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- 4 ounces sliced capicola (hot or sweet, to preference)
- 4 ounces sliced provolone
- 4 thick slices thick-cut, soft Italian sandwich bread, about 6 by 4 inches and not too crusty
- 1 ripe tomato, sliced

## Instructions

1. Toss cabbage with vinegar, olive oil, celery seed, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and sugar; let stand while preparing fries.
2. Cut unpeeled potatoes into fries. Fry in about 1 inch of vegetable oil over medium heat 8–10 minutes until cooked and crisp; drain and season with salt.
3. Sear capicola in a hot skillet about 1 minute per side. Stack it and provolone to begin melting.
4. Layer capicola and cheese on two bread slices, then add fries, slaw, tomato, and top bread. Cut and serve at once.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Slaw can stand while fries cook; total about 25–40 minutes by estimate.
