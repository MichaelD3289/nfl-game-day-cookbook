## Ingredients

### Pastry shells

- 2 cups margarine
- 3 1/4 cups flour, plus more for rolling
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup milk

### Cream filling

- 4 cups powdered sugar
- 1/2 pint whipping cream
- 3 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 3 cups margarine, at room temperature
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

## Instructions

1. Cream the margarine and sugar in a stand mixer 3 to 4 minutes, mix in the milk, then the flour and salt. Chill at least 2 hours or overnight.
2. Heat the oven to 350°F. Divide the dough into 4 portions. On a floured surface, roll each to 1/8 inch and cut strips about 1/2 inch wide and 10 inches long.
3. Wrap each strip around a lady locks mold (or a foil-wrapped clothespin), 2 inches apart on a baking sheet. Bake 13 to 15 minutes, until the shells just start to brown.
4. Cool 2 to 3 minutes, then slide the shells off the molds. Cool completely.
5. For the filling, beat the powdered sugar, cream and vanilla 5 minutes. Add half the margarine and beat 5 minutes; add the rest and beat about 5 minutes until light and fluffy.
6. Pipe the filling into the shells. Store airtight and dust with powdered sugar before serving.

## Kitchen Notes

You need lady locks molds or clothespins wrapped in foil. Do not let the shells cool fully on the molds or they crack and crumble.
