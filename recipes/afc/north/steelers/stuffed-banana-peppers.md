## Ingredients

### Filling

- 4 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 cup seasoned bread crumbs
- 2/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup minced fresh basil, or 4 teaspoons dried basil
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper
- 1/2 pound ground beef
- 1/2 pound bulk hot Italian sausage
- 1/2 pound ground veal

### Peppers and sauce

- 24 banana peppers
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 6 cups tomato sauce, divided
- 2/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese, for topping

## Instructions

1. Grease two 13x9-inch baking dishes and spread 1/2 cup of the tomato sauce in each. Wearing disposable gloves, cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds.
2. Combine the eggs, bread crumbs, 2/3 cup Parmesan, basil, garlic, salt and pepper in a large bowl. Add the beef, sausage and veal and mix thoroughly. Spoon the mixture into the peppers.
3. Brown the stuffed peppers in batches in the hot oil over medium heat, 1 to 2 minutes per side.
4. Arrange 12 peppers in each dish, cover with the remaining sauce and sprinkle with the remaining Parmesan.
5. Cover one dish and bake at 350°F for 35 to 40 minutes, until the meat is fully cooked and the peppers are tender. Freeze the other dish for up to 3 months, thaw overnight in the refrigerator, take it out 30 minutes before baking, and bake covered at 350°F for 45 to 50 minutes.

## Kitchen Notes

Sausage-stuffed banana peppers in red sauce appear on the menus of Pittsburgh Italian restaurants such as Cucina Vitale and Scoglio's Greentree. This version is from a Pittsburgh home cook and makes two dishes, so one can go in the freezer for a later game day.
