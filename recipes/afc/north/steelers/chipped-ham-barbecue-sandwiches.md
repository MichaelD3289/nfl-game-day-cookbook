## Ingredients

- 1 pound Isaly’s chipped chopped ham if available, or other ham shaved paper-thin at deli
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 1/2 cups ketchup
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 4 soft hamburger or Kaiser buns, split; use up to 6 smaller buns if preferred

## Instructions

1. Combine brown sugar, ketchup, vinegar, Worcestershire, salt, pepper, mustard, and onion powder to make the sauce.
2. Stir chipped ham into the sauce. Heat in a large skillet over medium heat for 15–20 minutes, until warmed through. Keep warm in a slow cooker if desired and serve on Kaiser rolls.
