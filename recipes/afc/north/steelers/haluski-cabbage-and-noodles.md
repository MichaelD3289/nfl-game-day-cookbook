## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
- 1 large yellow onion, sliced
- 1 1/2 pounds green cabbage, cored and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1–1 1/2 teaspoons sea salt, divided
- 1–1 1/2 teaspoons black pepper, divided
- 8 ounces wide egg noodles
- 2 green onions, chopped
- Optional: 1 (14-ounce) package kielbasa, sliced into 1/2-inch rings

### For serving

- Sour cream, optional

## Instructions

1. If using kielbasa, brown it in a Dutch oven over medium heat and set aside. Add 3 tablespoons butter; soften sliced onion for 3 minutes. Add cabbage in thirds, seasoning with 1 teaspoon each salt and pepper; add 2 more tablespoons butter as cabbage is added. Cover and cook on low 15–23 minutes, stirring often, until soft.
2. Meanwhile, cook egg noodles in salted water for one minute less than package directions; drain.
3. Fold in cooked kielbasa if using and add noodles with the remaining 3 tablespoons butter. Cook 5–6 minutes until hot and combined. Taste and add remaining salt and pepper if needed. Serve with green onions and optional sour cream.
