## Ingredients

### Dough

- 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus more for rolling
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 tablespoons neutral vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons cold water
- 1 large egg

### Filling and finish

- 10 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
- 2 large onions, chopped
- 1 pound russet potatoes, peeled and cut in 2-inch chunks
- 1 cup grated white cheddar (about 4 ounces)
- 1/4 cup sour cream, plus more to serve
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, divided, plus salt for cooking water

## Instructions

1. Stir flour and 1 teaspoon salt. Whisk sour cream, oil, 2 tablespoons cold water, and egg separately; combine with flour and knead 3–4 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, melt 4 tablespoons butter in a skillet. Cook onions with 1/2 teaspoon salt over medium heat 15–20 minutes until deep golden. Save 1/3 cup onions for filling; keep remaining onions in skillet.
3. Boil potatoes in salted water 15–18 minutes until tender. Drain; mash with cheddar, 1/4 cup sour cream, 4 tablespoons butter, 1 teaspoon salt, and reserved onions. Cool fully.
4. Roll half the dough very thin. Cut 3 1/2-inch rounds. Put about 1 tablespoon cool filling on each, wet rim lightly, fold in half, and pinch closed. Repeat with remaining dough and re-rolled scraps; keep pierogi on a floured tray.
5. Boil pierogi in salted water in batches. Once they float, cook another 3–4 minutes until dough is tender. Meanwhile melt remaining 2 tablespoons butter with skillet onions.
6. Lift pierogi into onion-butter skillet, toss gently and warm through. Serve with extra sour cream.

## Kitchen Notes

Prep: 1 hour 30 minutes active; 30-minute dough rest

Cook: About 30 minutes overlapping filling and boiling
