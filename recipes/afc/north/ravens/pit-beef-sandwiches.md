## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1 (3-pound) eye round beef roast
- 8 kaiser rolls or potato rolls, or 16 slices white sandwich bread
- 1/2 cup creamy horseradish sauce (prepared horseradish mixed with mayonnaise, if making it) {{component:baltimore-horseradish-sauce}}
- 1 large sweet onion, preferably Vidalia in season, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Mix salt, pepper, garlic powder, and chili powder; rub over beef. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
2. Bring beef to room temperature for about 30 minutes. Prepare a grill with direct and indirect heat; for gas use medium-high, about 450°F.
3. Sear beef over direct heat for 2–3 minutes per side until crusted, then move to indirect heat, cover, and grill 30–45 minutes to medium-rare (about 130°F internal).
4. Rest no more than 5 minutes, slice very thinly across the grain, and pile on rolls with horseradish sauce and onion.

## Kitchen Notes

Prep: At least 4 hours (includes meat seasoning rest)
