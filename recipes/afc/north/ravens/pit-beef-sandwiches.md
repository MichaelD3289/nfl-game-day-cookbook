## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon each dried oregano, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika and chili powder
- 1 (4-pound) bottom round roast (top round also works)
- 10 kaiser rolls
- 1 cup tiger sauce (creamy horseradish sauce) {{component:baltimore-horseradish-sauce}}
- 1 sweet white onion, thinly sliced

## Instructions

1. Mix salt, pepper, oregano, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika and chili powder. Season the beef all over, wrap in plastic and refrigerate at least 1 hour or overnight.
2. Light a chimney of charcoal and bank the lit coals on one side of the grill. Place beef on the cool side, cover with the top vent over the meat, and cook until the center reaches 110°F for rare or 120°F for medium-rare.
3. Move beef to the hot side and sear, turning every minute, until crusted on all sides and 120°F for rare or 125–130°F for medium-rare. Rest 10 minutes.
4. Slice as thinly as possible, ideally on a meat slicer. Pile onto rolls with onion and tiger sauce; serve immediately.

## Kitchen Notes

Baltimore stands cook seasoned top or bottom round over hot charcoal, keep the center rare to medium-rare, shave it thin and serve it on a kaiser roll with raw onion and horseradish sauce. Barbecue sauce is a common extra.
