## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 small onion, julienned
- 2 medium apples, chopped (Fuji, Pink Lady or Jonagold)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 8 ounces altbier or amber beer
- 16 ounces sauerkraut
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard (optional)
- 1 tablespoon caraway seeds (optional)

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a stockpot over medium heat. Add the onion and apples with a pinch of salt and pepper and cook 10 to 15 minutes, until the onion is translucent.
2. Pour in the beer and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
3. Add the whole jar of sauerkraut, the chicken stock, and the mustard and caraway if using.
4. Simmer until the liquid has reduced by about a third; simmer longer for a thicker result.

## Kitchen Notes

Long before the recent fermentation revival, sauerkraut was an important Thanksgiving tradition for generations of Baltimoreans. The source uses Baltimore's Union Craft Brewing Balt Altbier and HEX Ferments sauerkraut.
