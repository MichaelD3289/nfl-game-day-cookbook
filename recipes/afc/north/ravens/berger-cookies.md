## Ingredients

### Cookies

- 1/2 cup (113 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tablespoon milk
- 1/2 teaspoon imitation vanilla extract
- 1 2/3 cups (190 g) cake flour
- 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

### Frosting

- 1 cup (226 g) salted butter, softened
- 6 tablespoons (88 ml) light corn syrup
- 5 cups (625 g) powdered sugar
- 2/3 cup (67 g) natural unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 tablespoons (13 g) Dutch-process unsweetened cocoa powder
- 2 tablespoons heavy cream
- 2 teaspoons imitation vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F and line baking sheets. Cream unsalted butter and sugar; mix in egg, milk, and vanilla. Whisk cake flour, baking powder, and salt separately, then combine with wet ingredients.
2. Drop level tablespoon portions onto sheets, space apart, dampen fingertips and flatten. Bake 10–12 minutes until edges begin to color; cool.
3. Beat salted butter with corn syrup, gradually mix in powdered sugar and both cocoa powders, then add cream and vanilla. Warm frosting over a double boiler, stirring smooth.
4. Turn fully cooled cookies flat-side up. Spread about 1 1/2 tablespoons warm fudge frosting thickly on each flat underside; let set before stacking.
