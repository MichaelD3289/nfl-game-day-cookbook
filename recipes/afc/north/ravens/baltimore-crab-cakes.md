## Ingredients

- 2 slices soft white bread, crusts removed, finely crumbled
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 teaspoons Old Bay Classic Seafood Seasoning
- 2 teaspoons dried parsley flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon French’s Classic Yellow Mustard (or another plain yellow mustard)
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 pound fresh lump crabmeat, preferably Maryland blue crab, picked free of shell
- 2 to 3 tablespoons neutral vegetable oil, for pan-frying

## Instructions

1. Mix bread crumbs, mayonnaise, Old Bay, parsley, mustard, and beaten egg in a bowl.
2. Gently fold in crab without crushing the lumps. Shape into four cakes; handle lightly so they hold together without extra breading.
3. Heat 2–3 tablespoons oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Fry cakes about 5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Turn carefully with a broad spatula.
4. Serve promptly. Lemon is a serving option; avoid burying the crab under sauce.
