## Ingredients

- 1 cup cream cheese, softened
- 3/4 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup Frank's RedHot Original Cayenne Pepper Hot Sauce
- 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 4 1/2 teaspoons Old Bay Classic Seafood Seasoning
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1 cup blue crab claw meat
- 1 cup lump blue crab meat
- 3/4 cup shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
- Sliced French bread or crackers, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, mix everything except the crab, cheese and bread until combined.
3. Gently fold in the claw and lump crab meat.
4. Spread in an 8-inch square baking dish and sprinkle the Cheddar on top.
5. Bake uncovered about 15 minutes, until the cheese melts and the dip is hot through. Serve right away with French bread or crackers.

## Kitchen Notes

Old Bay, the Chesapeake seafood seasoning, is made by McCormick, which is headquartered just outside Baltimore.
