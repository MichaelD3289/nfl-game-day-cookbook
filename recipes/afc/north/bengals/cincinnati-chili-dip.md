## Ingredients

### Chili

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 cup plus 2 tablespoons minced white onion (reserve 2 tablespoons for garnish)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 ounce unsweetened baking chocolate
- 1 (6-ounce) can tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 4 cups beef broth

### Dip layers

- 2 (8-ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
- 1 jalapeño, minced (reserve half for garnish)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried minced garlic
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried minced onion
- 2 cups grated cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Cook 1 cup of the onion and the garlic for 2 minutes, then stir in the chili powder, cumin, oregano, cinnamon, allspice and cloves and cook for 1 minute.
2. Add the beef and cook about 5 minutes, until no longer pink. Stir in the chocolate, tomato paste, vinegar, Worcestershire sauce and broth and bring to a simmer.
3. Reduce the heat to medium-low and cook for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Heat the oven to 400°F. Mix the cream cheese, half the jalapeño, the dried garlic and the dried onion until smooth, and spread in a casserole dish.
5. Spoon the chili evenly over the cream cheese and sprinkle with the cheddar.
6. Bake 10 to 15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and bubbly. Garnish with the reserved onion and jalapeño.

## Kitchen Notes

Serve hot with chips for dipping. The chili is made the Cincinnati way, with cinnamon, allspice, cloves and unsweetened chocolate.
