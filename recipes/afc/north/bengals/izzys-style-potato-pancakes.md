## Ingredients

- 10 ounces white Kennebec potatoes, chopped
- 10 ounces white Kennebec potatoes, shredded
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 tablespoons milk
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- Cooking oil, for frying (1 to 2 inches deep)

## Instructions

1. Chop half the potatoes by hand or pulse them in a food processor. Shred the other half with the shredding disc. There is no need to peel or pat dry.
2. Stir the potatoes with the eggs, flour, milk, salt, pepper, garlic powder and onion powder.
3. Heat 1 to 2 inches of oil in a large skillet to about 350°F.
4. Scoop about 1/2 cup of the mixture into the oil and press it flat with the edge of a pancake turner.
5. Fry until the underside is browned, then flip and brown the other side. Drain on paper towels.

## Kitchen Notes

Izzy's is a Cincinnati deli, and it always serves its famous dill pickles alongside. This is a home copy, not the deli's own recipe. The pancakes come out thicker than regular latkes and are easy to eat by hand. Serve with ketchup or malt vinegar and a deli sandwich such as a Reuben.
