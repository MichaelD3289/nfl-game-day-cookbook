## Ingredients

### Peanut butter filling

- 2 cups sifted powdered sugar
- 3/4 cup smooth peanut butter
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) unsalted butter, melted
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon table salt

### Chocolate coating

- 12 ounces semisweet chocolate, finely chopped
- 1/2 teaspoon vegetable shortening

## Instructions

1. Line a baking sheet with parchment or waxed paper.
2. Beat the powdered sugar, peanut butter, butter, vanilla and salt in a large bowl with a wooden spoon, or knead by hand, until smooth and pliable but not sticky.
3. Roll into 1-inch balls and set them on the baking sheet. Freeze until firm, 15 to 20 minutes.
4. Melt the chocolate and shortening in a small heatproof bowl over a saucepan of simmering water, stirring often, until smooth. Remove from the heat.
5. Spear each ball with a thin skewer or toothpick and dip about three-quarters of it in the chocolate, leaving a small circle of peanut butter showing on top. Return to the sheet and remove the toothpick. Reheat the chocolate if needed.

## Kitchen Notes

The candies are made to look like the nut of the buckeye, Ohio's state tree. This is Ohio State University's traditional recipe.
