## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds boneless beef chuck, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 3/4 pound boneless pork shoulder, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 4 cups beef stock
- 4 cups water, for meat simmer
- Kosher salt, to taste
- 1 cup steel-cut (pinhead) oats; do not use rolled oats
- 3 1/3 cups water, for oats
- 1/4 cup dried onion flakes
- 1 1/4 teaspoons ground white pepper
- 1/4 cup canola oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. Line a 9-by-5-inch loaf pan with plastic wrap, leaving enough overhang to cover the filled pan.
2. Bring beef, pork, stock, 4 cups water, and salt to a boil in a large pot. Lower heat and simmer 1 1/2–2 hours until the meat is very tender. Strain, saving 1/3 cup cooking liquid, then pulse meat in a processor until finely ground.
3. While meat cooks, boil 3 1/3 cups water, add steel-cut oats and salt, then cook about 15 minutes until tender, stirring occasionally.
4. Process cooked oats with ground meat, reserved 1/3 cup liquid, onion flakes, and white pepper into a thick paste. Taste and add salt if needed. Pack firmly into loaf pan, cover, and chill overnight.
5. Unwrap, slice 1/2 inch thick, and fry in canola oil in batches over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes, turning once, until crisp and browned.

## Kitchen Notes

Cook: 1 1/2–2 hours simmer; 15 minutes oats; 4–5 minutes fry per batch
