## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour, plus extra for rolling
- ½ teaspoon salt
- 6 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, diced
- 2 tablespoons cold vegetable shortening
- 3 tablespoons ice water, plus more ½ tablespoon at a time if needed

### Filling

- 2 cups heavy whipping cream
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, diced
- ⅛ teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Pulse crust flour and salt in a food processor. Add cold butter and shortening; pulse to coarse crumbs with pea-size butter pieces. Add 3 tablespoons ice water and pulse just until dough gathers; add extra water ½ tablespoon at a time only if needed. Pat into a disk, wrap, and chill 1 hour to 1 day.
2. Roll dough to an 11-inch circle. Fit into a 9-inch pie plate, fold overhang under, crimp, and prick the bottom with a fork. Chill about 30 minutes until firm.
3. Place a baking sheet on the middle rack and heat oven to 425°F. Whisk cream, sugar, flour, and vanilla smooth. Pour into cold shell, dot with diced butter, and sprinkle with nutmeg.
4. Place pie on the hot sheet and bake 10 minutes. Lower oven to 350°F and bake about 55 minutes more, until crust is golden and filling bubbles in spots; center can still jiggle slightly. Shield crust edges with foil if darkening too quickly. Cool completely on a rack; serve chilled or at room temperature.
