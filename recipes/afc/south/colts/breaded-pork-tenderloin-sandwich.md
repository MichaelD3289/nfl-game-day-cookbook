## Ingredients

- 4 center-cut pork-loin cutlets, 5 ounces each
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 sleeves Ritz crackers, finely crushed
- 2 tablespoons garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon black pepper, plus a second 1-tablespoon “pepper” entry printed by source (likely duplicate; see note)
- 2 tablespoons salt
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 40 ounces vegetable oil for deep frying (use only enough for safe frying depth) {{no-scale}}
- 4 soft hamburger buns (assembly quantity inferred from four cutlets)
- Classic toppings to taste: dill pickle chips, sliced white onion, yellow mustard; lettuce, tomato, and mayonnaise optional

## Instructions

1. Butterfly each pork cutlet almost through and open it. Pound between sheets of wax paper to ¼–½ inch thick, wider than the bun.
2. Beat eggs with milk in a shallow dish. Crush Ritz crackers finely and mix with flour, garlic powder, salt, and the first listed tablespoon of black pepper in a second dish. The source repeats another tablespoon of “pepper”; add only if you want the extra heat.
3. Dip cutlets in egg mixture, then press into crumbs on both sides. Heat vegetable oil to 350°F in a deep, heavy pot; fill no more than halfway. Fry in batches until deeply golden and the center reaches at least 145°F, allowing a 3-minute rest before eating. Drain on a rack.
4. Place each oversized cutlet on a soft bun. Add pickle, thin white onion and yellow mustard for a classic diner profile; add lettuce, tomato or mayonnaise to personal taste.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: Pounding/coating several cutlets and frying batches vary; thermometer to 350°F before frying.
