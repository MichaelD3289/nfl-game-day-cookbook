## Ingredients

- Rub: 1 tablespoon paprika
- 2 teaspoons light brown sugar
- 1½ teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon chipotle chile powder
- ½ teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, for rub
- 1½ pounds skirt steak (the traditional fajita cut)
- 1 tablespoon olive oil and juice of 1 lime, for steak
- Vegetables: 2 red and 2 yellow bell peppers, cut into 1-inch strips
- 2 large red onions, thickly sliced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil and juice of 2 limes, for vegetables
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, for vegetables
- Canola or other neutral oil, for grill grates
- Warm flour tortillas, for serving; number to appetite
- Optional toppings: pickled jalapeños, lime wedges, chopped scallions, or shredded cheddar
- Optional: guacamole, homemade or refrigerated prepared {{component:quick-guacamole}}
- Optional: fresh pico de gallo, homemade or deli prepared {{component:fresh-pico-de-gallo}}

## Instructions

1. Combine paprika, brown sugar, chili powder, cumin, chipotle powder, garlic powder, and 1 teaspoon kosher salt. Toss skirt steak with 1 tablespoon oil and the juice of 1 lime; rub spice mix on both sides. Cover and refrigerate 30 minutes to 2 hours.
2. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Toss peppers and onions with 3 tablespoons oil, juice of 2 limes, and 1 teaspoon kosher salt. Cook in batches, stirring occasionally, until tender and charred at the edges, 8–10 minutes; keep warm.
3. Heat grill to medium-high and oil grates. Grill thicker skirt pieces 4–5 minutes per side, or thin pieces 2–3 minutes per side, aiming for medium-rare. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly across the grain.
4. Serve sliced steak and vegetables with warm flour tortillas. Offer guacamole and salsa; cheddar, sour cream, and pickled jalapeños are personal options.

Prep: 35 minutes active; 30 minutes to 2 hours marinating
