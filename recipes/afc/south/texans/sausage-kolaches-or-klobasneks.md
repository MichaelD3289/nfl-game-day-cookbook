## Ingredients

### Dough

- 1 cup (227 g) sour cream
- ½ cup (99 g) granulated sugar
- 1½ teaspoons (9 g) table salt
- 8 tablespoons (113 g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 teaspoons instant yeast
- ½ cup (113 g) lukewarm water
- 2 large eggs
- 4 cups (482 g) all-purpose flour

### Filling

- 20 small cooked breakfast sausage links, about 2½ inches long
- Optional shredded cheddar and yellow mustard, to taste; melted butter for brushing

## Instructions

1. Warm sour cream gently, then mix with sugar, salt and melted butter; let cool to lukewarm so it will not harm the yeast. Stir in yeast, water, eggs and flour. Knead until smooth.
2. Cover and refrigerate the dough overnight. Divide into 20 equal pieces. Flatten each into an oval, put one fully cooked, cooled sausage link in its center and add optional cheddar or mustard. Wrap and pinch seam closed; place seam down on a baking sheet.
3. Cover and let rise about 1 hour until puffy. Heat oven to 350°F. Bake 15–20 minutes until golden; brush with melted butter if desired.
