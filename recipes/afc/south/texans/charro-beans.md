## Ingredients

- 1 lb. dried pinto beans, rinsed and soaked
- 4 cloves garlic
- 4 slices of bacon, diced into small pieces
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 serrano chiles, chopped
- 1 tomato, diced
- 1 tablespoon salt
- Cilantro, chopped, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Put the beans and garlic in a large pot, cover with about 2 inches of water and boil over high heat for about 2 hours. After the first hour, check the water and add 1 to 2 cups of warm water if needed.
2. Add the salt about 30 minutes before the beans finish cooking.
3. Lightly brown the bacon in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and chiles and sauté until the onion is golden, 5 to 6 minutes. Stir in the tomato and remove from the heat.
4. When the beans are tender, stir in the bacon mixture and simmer for 10 more minutes.
5. Garnish with chopped cilantro and, if you like, avocado.
