## Ingredients

- 2½ gallons water
- 1 cup Zatarain’s Crawfish, Shrimp & Crab Boil seasoning (or Old Bay for a milder, celery-salt-forward profile)
- 2 lemongrass stalks, ends trimmed
- ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
- 2 heads garlic, halved
- ½ cup kosher salt, divided in two ¼-cup portions
- 1½ pounds red potatoes, quartered
- 4 pounds live crawfish, purged and rinsed; discard dead crawfish
- 3 ears sweet corn, halved

### Butter sauce

- 1 cup (2 sticks) salted butter
- 2 tablespoons minced lemongrass
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon ground cayenne

## Instructions

1. Use a very large stockpot. Combine water, seafood-boil seasoning, whole lemongrass, ¼ cup lemon juice, halved garlic heads, and ¼ cup kosher salt. Bring to a covered boil. Add potatoes; cook 10–15 minutes until nearly tender.
2. Add remaining ¼ cup salt and return to a rolling boil. Add live crawfish and corn; boil about 8 minutes until crawfish are bright red and cooked through.
3. During the boil, melt salted butter over low heat; add minced lemongrass and garlic and warm until fragrant without browning. Stir in 2 tablespoons lemon juice and cayenne.
4. Drain the seafood and vegetables well, transfer to a wide serving bowl, toss with garlicky butter sauce, and serve immediately.
