## Ingredients

- 3 medium-sized green tomatoes, coarsely chopped
- 4 tomatillos, husked and chopped
- 1 to 2 jalapeños, stemmed and coarsely chopped
- 3 small garlic cloves
- 3 medium-sized ripe avocados, peeled, pitted and sliced
- 4 sprigs cilantro
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 1/2 cups sour cream

## Instructions

1. Put the green tomatoes, tomatillos, jalapeños and garlic in a saucepan and bring to a boil. The tomatoes supply the liquid.
2. Reduce the heat and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes, then remove from the heat and let cool slightly.
3. Blend the tomato mixture with the avocados, cilantro and salt until smooth.
4. Transfer to a bowl and stir in the sour cream. Serve with tortilla chips.

## Kitchen Notes

Ninfa Laurenzo, proprietor of the Houston chain Ninfa's, is credited with inventing green sauce. If green tomatoes are unavailable, use 6 more tomatillos.
