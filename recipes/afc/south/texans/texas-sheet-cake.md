## Ingredients

### Cake

- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup cocoa
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 cup butter (1 stick), melted
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup canola or other vegetable oil
- 1 cup water
- 2 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Frosting

- 1/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons milk
- 1/4 cup cocoa
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1 pound confectioners sugar, sifted (about 4 cups)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F and grease and flour a 13x9x2-inch pan.
2. Sift together the sugar, flour, cocoa, baking soda and cinnamon.
3. Combine the melted butter, buttermilk, oil, water, eggs and vanilla, then mix with the dry ingredients into a smooth, fairly thin batter.
4. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
5. While the cake bakes, heat the milk and cocoa in a saucepan, add the butter and stir over medium heat until melted. Remove from the heat and stir in the confectioners sugar and vanilla until smooth, then add the pecans.
6. Spread the frosting over the cake as soon as it comes out of the oven.
