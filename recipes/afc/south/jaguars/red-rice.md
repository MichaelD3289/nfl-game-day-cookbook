## Ingredients

- 4 cups Carolina Gold rice or jasmine (rinsed until water is clear)
- 1.5 pounds smoked sausage or smoked turkey sausage, sliced
- 4 slices bacon, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 green bell pepper, diced
- 3 ribs celery, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 6 tablespoons tomato paste
- 5 cups chicken or seafood stock
- 2 teaspoons sea salt
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 3 teaspoons paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 tablespoons oil and bacon drippings

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. In a heavy Dutch oven, heat the oil and brown the sausage and bacon. Remove and set aside.
2. Sauté the onion, bell pepper and celery until softened, 5 to 7 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 30 seconds. Add half of the seasoning.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook until slightly darkened to deepen the flavor.
4. Add the crushed tomatoes, stock, remaining seasonings and the meat. Bring to a gentle boil.
5. Stir in the rinsed rice and bring back to a gentle boil. Do not over stir.
6. Cover tightly and bake 35 to 40 minutes, until the rice is tender. Let rest covered 10 minutes, then fluff gently with a fork and serve.

## Kitchen Notes

Traditionally served with fried fish, smothered shrimp, fried chicken or greens, or on its own. Jacksonville is the southernmost point of the Gullah Geechee Cultural Heritage Corridor.
