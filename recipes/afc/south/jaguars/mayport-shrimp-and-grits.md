## Ingredients

- 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined; fresh Mayport shrimp when available
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon Old Bay seasoning
- ¼ cup water
- Juice of 1 large lemon
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 4 servings creamy stone-ground grits, homemade or prepared Original Creamy Gracious Grits to package directions {{component:creamy-stone-ground-grits}}
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Make four servings of stone-ground grits using the recipe, or prepare four servings of Original Creamy Gracious Grits by its package directions. Keep warm; pat shrimp dry and prepare the remaining ingredients.
2. Heat olive oil and butter in a 10–12-inch skillet over medium heat. Add sliced garlic; stir about 30 seconds, just until fragrant. Add shrimp and season with Old Bay, salt and black pepper.
3. Sauté about 2 minutes, turning shrimp. Add lemon juice, ¼ cup water and parsley; cook roughly 2 minutes more until shrimp are opaque and 145°F if checked with a thermometer.
4. Spoon hot grits onto plates, top with shrimp and pan juices, then finish with Parmesan.

## Kitchen Notes

Start homemade grits first; prepare the shrimp ingredients while they simmer and cook the shrimp just before serving. Allow about 45–60 minutes overall (estimated), depending on the grind. Prepared Gracious Grits follows the original Jacksonville recipe and can shorten the time; follow its package directions.
