## Ingredients

- 4 soft pita pockets, 6–7 inches across
- 1/2 pound thinly sliced deli ham
- 1/2 pound thinly sliced salami
- 1/2 pound thinly sliced bologna
- 4 slices American cheese
- 1 medium ripe tomato, thinly sliced
- About 2 cups shredded lettuce
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise (about 2 tablespoons per pita)
- 1/4 cup Italian or oil-and-vinegar dressing (about 1 tablespoon per pita)
- Optional: 1/4 cup sliced mild banana peppers

## Instructions

1. Trim about 2 inches from one end of each pita and gently open the pocket. Warm briefly only if stiff, so the bread bends without tearing.
2. Spread about 2 tablespoons mayonnaise inside each pita. Divide ham, salami, bologna, cheese, tomato and lettuce evenly among the four pockets.
3. Drizzle about 1 tablespoon dressing into each pocket. Add banana peppers if desired and serve right away.
