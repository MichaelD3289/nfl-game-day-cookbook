## Ingredients

### Meatloaf

- ½ large onion, chopped
- 3 celery ribs, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 teaspoons black pepper
- 2 teaspoons granulated garlic
- ¼ cup beef broth
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- ½ cup plain bread crumbs
- 2½ pounds 80%-lean ground chuck
- Nonstick spray

### Tomato Creole sauce

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- ½ cup diced onion
- 1 celery rib, diced
- 1 garlic clove, smashed
- 4 cups ketchup
- 1 tablespoon prepared horseradish
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 chopped tomato
- 1 teaspoon each black pepper, salt and granulated garlic
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

### Turnip greens (6 side portions)

- 1 pound turnip greens, washed and chopped
- 2 slices bacon, chopped
- ½ small onion, diced
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon cider vinegar
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Baked macaroni and cheese (6 side portions)

- 8 ounces elbow macaroni
- 8 ounces shredded cheddar
- ⅔ cup milk
- ¼ cup melted butter
- ½ cup crushed saltine crackers
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Southern green beans (6 side portions)

- 2 pounds green beans, trimmed (fresh or frozen)
- 4 slices bacon, chopped
- ½ small onion, diced
- 1 cup chicken broth
- ¼ teaspoon black pepper
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix meatloaf onion, celery, garlic, salt, pepper, granulated garlic, broth, Worcestershire, eggs and crumbs. Gently mix in beef, shape into a loaf on a sprayed rimmed pan and bake about 50 minutes, until center reaches 165°F. Rest 10 minutes.
2. For sauce, heat olive oil in a saucepan. Soften onion, celery and garlic 5–10 minutes. Stir in remaining sauce ingredients and simmer 10 minutes, stirring often. Serve sauce with sliced meatloaf; the source's full ketchup quantity makes extra sauce.
3. For greens, cook bacon and onion in a pot until onion softens. Add greens and broth; cover and simmer 30–40 minutes until tender. Stir in vinegar and season to taste.
4. For macaroni, cook pasta in salted water until just tender and drain. Layer half the pasta and cheddar in a greased baking dish; repeat. Season with salt and pepper. Mix crackers with melted butter and scatter on top; pour milk evenly over the dish. Bake at 350°F for 35–45 minutes, until set; rest 10 minutes.
5. For green beans, cook bacon in a saucepan until lightly browned. Add onion and soften 3 minutes. Add beans, broth and pepper; cover and simmer 25–35 minutes until tender. Season to taste.
6. Serve each plate with one slice of meatloaf and ½ cup each of turnip greens, baked macaroni and cheese, and Southern green beans. The sides can cook while the meatloaf bakes.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: The entrée and sauce are Kahlil Arnold's meatloaf from Arnold's Country Kitchen. The three measured sides make this a complete plate; they are homestyle pairings, not represented as Arnold's exact daily side recipes. The 4-cup ketchup sauce quantity is retained as printed and yields extra.
