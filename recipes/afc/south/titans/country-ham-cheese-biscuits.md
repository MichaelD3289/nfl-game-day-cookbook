## Ingredients

- 2 cups unbleached self-rising flour
- ¼ cup shortening
- 1 cup finely chopped cooked country ham
- 1 cup shredded Swiss cheese
- ¼ cup milk or buttermilk
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2½ tablespoons butter or margarine, melted

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 450°F.
2. Add the flour to a medium-to-large bowl and work in the shortening until the mix looks like coarse crumbs.
3. Stir in the ham, cheese, milk or buttermilk and sour cream until fully combined.
4. Knead the dough lightly on a floured surface for a few strokes.
5. Roll the dough about ½ inch thick and cut out biscuits, pressing the cutter straight down without twisting.
6. Place on an ungreased baking sheet and bake 8–10 minutes. Brush the tops with the melted butter and serve warm.
