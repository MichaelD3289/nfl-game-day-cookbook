## Ingredients

### Pimiento cheese

- 8 ounces shredded Cheddar cheese
- 4 ounces pasteurized process cheese spread
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup salad dressing
- 1 (4-ounce) jar diced pimiento, drained
- 2 1/4 teaspoons sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon black pepper

### Fried tomatoes

- 1/2 cup yellow cornmeal
- 1/2 cup flour
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 4 green tomatoes, sliced 1/4" thick
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- 4 slices bacon, fried and crumbled
- Oil, about 1/2" deep in a skillet, for frying

## Instructions

1. In a mixer, combine the cheeses and mayonnaise. Add the salad dressing, pimiento, sugar, salt and pepper and set aside.
2. In a flat bowl, combine the cornmeal, flour, salt, pepper and cayenne.
3. Heat about 1/2" oil in a skillet.
4. Dredge the tomato slices in the buttermilk, then in the flour mixture.
5. Fry in the hot oil until golden on one side, then turn and fry until golden on the other.
6. Top each slice with a tablespoon of the pimiento cheese (some will be left over), sprinkle with bacon and serve warm.
