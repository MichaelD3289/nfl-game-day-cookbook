## Ingredients

### Nougat

- 3 egg whites, room temperature
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 575 grams (3 cups) granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup light corn syrup
- 1/4 cup water

### Caramel

- 100 mL sweetened condensed milk
- 65 grams unsalted butter, chopped
- 65 grams granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon light corn syrup or golden syrup
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3/4 cup salted roasted peanuts, chopped

### Coating

- 12 ounces good-quality milk chocolate, chopped

## Instructions

1. Line a rimmed baking sheet. For nougat, heat honey, corn syrup, water, and granulated sugar to 310°F. Remove from heat; allow temperature to rise to 320°F, then cool to 300°F before adding to egg whites.
2. Beat egg whites with salt and powdered sugar to soft peaks. While mixing, pour hot syrup in a thin stream, avoiding the beaters. Continue whipping until very thick. Spread on a cornstarch-dusted surface, transfer to pan, cool several hours, and cut into rounds. The source batch makes about twice the nougat needed for 12 clusters.
3. For caramel, cook condensed milk, butter, sugar, corn syrup and salt, stirring, until 250°F. Remove from heat and add vanilla. Spoon onto nougat rounds and add chopped salted roasted peanuts. Let set 2–4 hours.
4. For a glossy shell, temper milk chocolate: melt two-thirds to about 113°F, stir in the remaining third and cool to about 81°F, then gently rewarm to 84–86°F. Dip the set clusters, let excess drain, and cool on parchment. For an easier coating, use milk-chocolate melting wafers according to the package; the shell will have a different texture.

## Kitchen Notes

Timing note: This is a fully from-scratch interpretation. The source makes the nougat and caramel, but its honey nougat is firmer than the commercial marshmallow nougat. Milk chocolate matches the original candy; its tempering temperatures follow Callebaut guidance. A purchased Original Goo Goo is the fastest option.
