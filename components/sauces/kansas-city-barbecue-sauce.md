## Ingredients

- 1 can (15 ounces) tomato sauce
- ⅓ cup packed brown sugar
- ¼ cup dark molasses
- ¼ cup apple cider vinegar
- ½ cup apricot preserves
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon each ground cumin, granulated garlic, and ground coriander
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## From Scratch

1. Combine everything in a nonreactive saucepan and whisk smooth.
2. Simmer gently, uncovered, for 30–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until glossy and spoon-coating.
3. Taste and adjust sweetness, vinegar, salt or black pepper. Measure the amount needed by the recipe, refrigerating the rest.

## Note

Make one batch for barbecue chicken and burnt ends, then refrigerate the remainder.
