## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound tomatillos, husked, rinsed, and halved
- 1/4 cup white onion
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 serrano pepper, stemmed and seeded
- Canola oil for drizzling
- 1 lime
- 1 teaspoon salt, then adjust to taste

## From Scratch

1. Heat oven to 450°F. Spread tomatillos, onion, garlic, and serrano on a lined sheet; drizzle lightly with oil.
2. Roast 10 minutes, toss, and roast 5 minutes more.
3. Blend roasted vegetables with salt and juice from half the lime. Taste and adjust lime or salt.

## Note

Roast the vegetables while the burrito rice or beans cook.
