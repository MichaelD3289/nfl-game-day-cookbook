## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon each garlic powder and onion powder

## From Scratch

1. Whisk milk, flour, soy sauce, Worcestershire, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder until no flour lumps remain.
2. Bring broth to a low boil. Slowly whisk in milk mixture; cook 1–2 minutes, stirring, until thick.
3. Use this sauce in place of both condensed soup cans; pour over the beef and vegetables before arranging tots.

## Note

Allow about 10 minutes (estimated). This is the source’s homemade cream sauce, not a mushroom-soup clone. For mushroom flavor, sauté 8 ounces finely chopped cremini mushrooms in 1 tablespoon butter until their liquid evaporates; fold into the sauce. That variation adds about 10 minutes.
