## Ingredients

- Reserved browned bits and defatted juices from the roast pan
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 2 cups low-sodium beef stock
- 1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme
- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste

## From Scratch

1. After moving roast to its rest, spoon off excess fat from the pan but keep brown bits and juices. Put pan over medium heat.
2. Pour in wine; scrape the pan and simmer until reduced by about half.
3. Add beef stock and simmer until reduced by about one-third to one-half. Stir in thyme, strain if desired, and season to taste.

## Note

Taste before salting; roast drippings can already be salty.
