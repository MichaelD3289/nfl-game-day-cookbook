## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Frank’s RedHot Original Cayenne Pepper Sauce (plain hot sauce, not bottled Buffalo Wings Sauce)
- 1/3 cup unsalted butter, melted

## From Scratch

1. Whisk the plain cayenne hot sauce into melted butter until uniform.
2. Taste and add a little more hot sauce for heat or butter for a milder coating. Toss fully cooked, hot wings just before serving.

## Note

Frank's Original is plain cayenne hot sauce. Mixing it with butter makes the classic wing sauce; premixed wing sauce is the shortcut.
