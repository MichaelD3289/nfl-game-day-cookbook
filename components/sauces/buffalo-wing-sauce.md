## Ingredients

- 2/3 cup Frank's RedHot Original cayenne sauce
- 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 tablespoons white vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne
- 1/8 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt, to taste

## From Scratch

1. Whisk everything over medium heat.
2. At the first edge bubbles, remove from heat; whisk smooth. Toss with cooked wings.

## Note

Allow 10 minutes. Chef John's variation; not a verified Anchor Bar formula. Plain hot sauce and butter also make a classic Buffalo sauce. Bottled Buffalo Wings Sauce is already blended; do not add this recipe's butter to it.
