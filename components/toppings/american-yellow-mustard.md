## Ingredients

- 1 cup cold water
- 3/4 cup yellow dry mustard powder
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/8 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup distilled white vinegar

## From Scratch

1. Whisk water, mustard powder, turmeric, garlic powder, paprika, and salt smooth in a saucepan.
2. Cook over medium-low heat about 10 minutes, stirring, until thickened. Whisk in vinegar and cook briefly to your preferred consistency.
3. Cool, then refrigerate. The sharp flavor mellows as it chills.

## Note

Allow about 15–20 minutes active cooking (estimated), then refrigerate a few days for the sharpness to mellow. This is refrigerated mustard, not a tested canning recipe.
