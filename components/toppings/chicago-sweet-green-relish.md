## Ingredients

- 3 green bell peppers, 2 cucumbers, and 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 tablespoon mustard seed
- 1 teaspoon celery salt
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 1 cup white-wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup water
- Optional green food coloring for the neon color

## From Scratch

1. Drain chopped vegetables, toss with salt, and refrigerate overnight; drain again.
2. Simmer vegetables with mustard seed, celery salt, turmeric, sugar, vinegar, and water for 10 minutes.
3. Cool and add food coloring dropwise only if desired. Use about 1 tablespoon per dog; refrigerate. This is not a canning formula.

## Note

Allow about 20 minutes active work plus overnight draining and cooling. The source leaves the salting quantity unspecified; 1 teaspoon is this book’s measured starting point. Neon color is optional. Keep refrigerated; this is not a canning recipe.
