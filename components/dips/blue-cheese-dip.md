## Ingredients

- 4 ounces crumbled blue cheese (Maytag for mild; Roquefort for stronger)
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup buttermilk
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice for a thicker wing dip; use up to 2 tablespoons for a pourable dressing
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## From Scratch

1. Mash blue cheese with sour cream until chunky and creamy.
2. Stir in buttermilk, mayonnaise and lemon juice. Taste and season with salt and black pepper.
3. Chill until serving; refrigerate covered for up to one week.

## Note

Use a mild blue cheese for a softer flavor or a strong one for more bite. Thin with a little more buttermilk if you want a pourable dressing.
