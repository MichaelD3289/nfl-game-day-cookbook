## Ingredients

- 2 ripe Hass avocados
- 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice, plus more to taste
- ¼ teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Optional 2 tablespoons finely chopped white onion and 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro

## From Scratch

1. Mash avocado coarsely with lime juice and salt.
2. Fold in optional onion and cilantro; taste and adjust lime and salt. Serve promptly.

## Note

Serve promptly, or press wrap against the surface to slow browning.
