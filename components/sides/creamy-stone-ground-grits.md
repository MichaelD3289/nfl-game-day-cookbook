## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 1 cup stone-ground grits, not instant
- 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, cubed
- 3/4 cup heavy cream, divided
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## From Scratch

1. Bring water and salt to a boil in a heavy saucepan. Whisk in the grits in a slow stream; reduce heat to a steady gentle simmer and partially cover.
2. Cook about 15 minutes, stirring frequently so the bottom does not catch. Uncover and stir in butter and 3 tablespoons cream.
3. Continue cooking and stirring about 15 minutes, adding the remaining cream a few tablespoons at a time. Taste a spoonful: the grains should be tender, without a hard center. Coarser grits may take longer; add hot water a little at a time if the mixture thickens before the grains soften.
4. Season with pepper and additional salt if needed. Keep warm until the shrimp are ready, loosening with a little hot water if necessary.

## Note

Allow about 10 minutes preparation and 30–40 minutes cooking; the source lists 37 minutes cooking. This is Paula Deen’s Southern butter-and-cream preparation, offered as a homemade alternative to the Jacksonville recipe’s purchased grits. It is not the manufacturer’s formula. Omit extra cheese here because the shrimp recipe already includes Parmesan.
