## Ingredients

- 1 cup stone-ground grits (white or yellow)
- 4½ cups water
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, or to taste
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- Optional: ¾ cup grated sharp cheddar for a richer cheese-grits version

## From Scratch

1. Bring the water and salt to a boil in a heavy saucepan. Whisk in grits slowly to avoid clumps, then lower heat to a gentle simmer.
2. Cook uncovered, stirring every few minutes and scraping the bottom, until tender and creamy, about 35–40 minutes; add a splash of hot water if they become too thick.
3. Remove from heat and stir in butter. Fold in cheddar only if desired; the main Mayport card already finishes the plated shrimp with Parmesan. Keep warm, adding a little hot water if needed.

## Note

Stone-ground grits take longer than ready-made grits; plan roughly 40 extra minutes.
