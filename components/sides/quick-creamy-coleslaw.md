## Ingredients

- 5 cups thinly sliced green cabbage
- 1 cup grated carrot
- 3/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 teaspoon celery salt
- Salt and pepper to taste

## From Scratch

1. Toss cabbage and carrot. Whisk mayonnaise, sour cream, vinegar, mustard, honey and celery salt.
2. Coat vegetables, season, and refrigerate 30 minutes before using.

## Note

Drain any excess liquid before piling slaw on the sandwich.
